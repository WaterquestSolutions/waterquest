Our Story

Why WaterQuest
Exists

WaterQuest Solutions was founded by a team of visionary engineers and environmental advocates with a single conviction: that clean hydraulic power should not come at the cost of environmental integrity.

We specialize in hydraulic systems that operate on tap water or seawater , without additives, without petroleum, and without compromise. Every system we deliver is built around 316 stainless steel and carbon-reinforced PEEK , materials selected not just for their individual properties, but for how they work together to resist corrosion, eliminate galling, and thrive in the harshest industrial environments.

From municipal wastewater gates to offshore marine systems, our work is guided by one principle: water is the most sustainable hydraulic medium on the planet, and industry deserves to use it.

"We don't just create products , we engineer solutions that contribute to a greener planet and a more sustainable industrial future."

Engineering Excellence

Materials Made
for Water

Two materials, engineered to work together. The pair that makes oil-free hydraulics not just possible , but superior.

316

Metal Component

316 Stainless Steel

Wetted metal surfaces throughout all components
Exceptional resistance to chlorides, H₂S, and saltwater
No dissimilar-metal traps , eliminating galvanic corrosion risk
Long service life in buried vaults, marine, and wastewater environments
Structural integrity maintained under high-pressure continuous duty
PEEK

Tribological Interface

Carbon-Reinforced PEEK

Deployed at every sliding and contact interface
Paired tribologically with 316 SS , PEEK on one surface, stainless on the other
Eliminates galling without oil lubrication
High compressive strength; resists deformation under load
Chemically inert to chlorine, bromine, acids, and solvents

The design principle: water provides fluid power , stainless and PEEK carry the tribology.
No oil. No additives. No compromise.
