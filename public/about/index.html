Our Story

Why we put water to work.

A gate needs to move. A valve needs to respond. A production line needs to keep running. When hydraulic oil adds leaks, clean-up or servicing to those jobs, we believe the working medium deserves a fresh look.

We specialise in purpose-built water hydraulic components and systems. We connect the load and operating sequence to the pump, actuator, valves and supporting equipment, so the proposal starts with the problem your team needs to solve.

From treatment-plant gates to marine equipment, our purpose is to make water a practical engineering choice: useful hydraulic performance, less working oil to manage and clear information for the people who select and maintain the system.

Engineering Excellence

Materials that make water service practical.

Water carries the power. The materials at loaded and sliding contacts make that power useful: select the metal, polymer, seal and surface together for the actual water and duty.

316

Metal Component

316 Stainless Steel

Wetted metal surfaces throughout all components
Exceptional resistance to chlorides, H₂S, and saltwater
No dissimilar-metal traps, eliminating galvanic corrosion risk
Long service life in buried vaults, marine, and wastewater environments
Structural integrity maintained under high-pressure continuous duty
PEEK

Tribological Interface

Carbon-Reinforced PEEK

Deployed at every sliding and contact interface
Paired tribologically with 316 SS, PEEK on one surface, stainless on the other
Eliminates galling without oil lubrication
High compressive strength; resists deformation under load
Chemically inert to chlorine, bromine, acids, and solvents

Review the complete wetted path, including fittings and connections, so the material choices work together throughout the circuit.
