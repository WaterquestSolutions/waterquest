Wqs Stainless

It's a small fitting. Until it's the reason your entire system is down.

The wrong thread, the wrong material or one overlooked connection can create leaks, premature corrosion and expensive rework. Specify 316 stainless steel fittings and adapters with confidence, using clear connection information and practical selection support to help get the installation right the first time.

Fittings and adapters

Match both connection ends, thread form, sealing method and working pressure. Similar nominal sizes can use different thread standards and cannot be assumed interchangeable.

Instrumentation fittings

Specify tube material, outside diameter, wall thickness and the fitting system. Follow the selected manufacturer’s assembly instructions and avoid mixing components without confirmed compatibility.

Pipe and pipe fittings

Specify pipe dimensions, wall thickness, connection method and pressure-temperature requirements. Assess corrosion against the actual medium and exposure, including cleaning agents and stagnant sections.

TrAle LOK twin ferrule

Match the twin-ferrule fitting to the specified tube material, diameter and wall thickness. Follow the manufacturer’s installation instructions and confirm compatibility of every part of the connection.

Flow measuring instruments

Select the measurement range, accuracy requirement and wetted materials for the fluid and flow regime. Check installation length, orientation, calibration and signal interfaces before comparing instruments.

Accessories and hardware

Complete the connection schedule with the required supporting hardware. Identify material, dimensions, quantity and traceability requirements so accessories meet the same installation conditions as the primary components.

Choose 316 stainless for a defined exposure

Molybdenum-bearing stainless grades can offer useful corrosion resistance, but no grade is suitable for every chloride, chemical or temperature condition. Review the actual medium, crevices, cleaning regime and connections to other metals. Obtain the material and product documentation required by the project.

Hydrogen and other demanding services need explicit confirmation

For hydrogen, gas, food-contact, high-pressure or regulated service, confirm the exact product’s permitted medium and pressure-temperature range. Review leakage criteria, material compatibility and applicable approval requirements. A generic fitting catalogue does not establish suitability for every specialised duty.

Ask for documents that answer the procurement question

Confirm the manufacturer and part number, current drawing, rating, connection standard and available material records. Product-specific documentation matters more than a general list of standards. For Dicsa or other manufacturer components, request the records relevant to the proposed supply.
