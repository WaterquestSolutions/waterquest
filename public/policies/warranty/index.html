Warranty Policy

Written coverage, claim information and the terms that govern your equipment purchase.

Policy overview

WaterQuest Solutions provides written warranty terms for the equipment and systems we supply. Your quotation, order confirmation or supply agreement identifies the applicable coverage, warranty period and claim conditions.

Coverage may differ between products and manufacturers. Where equipment carries a manufacturer’s warranty, we will identify the applicable terms and explain our role in supporting your claim.

Requesting warranty support

Email bruce@waterquestsolutions.com with:

  • Your company name and purchase reference.
  • The equipment model and serial number.
  • A description of the issue and when it occurred.
  • Available photographs, operating data and maintenance records.

We will review the information, explain the next steps and advise whether inspection or return of the equipment is required. Please contact us before returning equipment or arranging repairs for which you intend to seek warranty reimbursement.

Responsibility for assessment, freight, removal, reinstallation and site attendance will be determined under the applicable warranty terms. Any proposed chargeable work will be explained before it proceeds.

This policy provides general guidance. Your applicable warranty and supply agreement govern your purchase. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
