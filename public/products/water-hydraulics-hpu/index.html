The Problem with Oil-Driven
Power Packs

💰

Financial Risk

Financial

Spill cleanup, regulatory fines, and remediation costs escalate quickly. Every facility that has relied on an oil-driven power pack has faced the same issue: leaks, spills, and the inevitable cleanup and reporting that follow.

📋

Compliance Risk

Compliance

Reputational Risk

Reputational

Contamination incidents damage public trust and ESG standing. The cost is not just financial. Oil contamination near water assets threatens compliance, public trust, and long-term infrastructure health.

Our Answer: Water Hydraulics HPUs

WaterQuest water hydraulics HPUs deliver the same power output as traditional oil packs but run 100% oil-free. Constructed entirely from 316L stainless steel and fitted with carbon-reinforced PEEK wear surfaces, these compact skids provide reliable hydraulic energy without risk of contamination. Whether operating on tap or seawater, they supply steady, efficient power while meeting today's strict environmental and ESG requirements.

Key Features

Built for Water.
Engineered for Performance.

316L Stainless Steel Construction

Every wetted component resists corrosion, even in high-chloride seawater or wastewater environments. No oil to degrade seals, no contamination risk to process streams.

Carbon-Reinforced PEEK Surfaces

Eliminates galling, reduces friction, and extends service intervals by preventing metal-to-metal wear throughout the power unit without requiring oil lubrication.

High-Efficiency Pumps and Motors

Integrated into the skid to ensure consistent performance with minimal input power. WaterQuest Solutions axial piston pumps deliver smooth, ripple-free pressure at up to 160 bar.

Accumulator Storage Options

Smooths out demand spikes, maintains steady pressure, and reduces energy draw. Optional accumulator integration available for applications with intermittent high-demand cycles.

Standard and Custom Skid Layouts

Configurations range from compact modular packs built on the Pick a Pack system to full custom builds for specific site requirements and integration needs.

Water-Only Operation

Runs on tap or seawater with no oil, no glycols, and no additives required. WRAS 1999 compliant tanks with overflow fitted as standard. ISO 9001 certified.

The Case for Water HPUs

Five Reasons to Choose
a Water Hydraulics HPU

🌊

Eliminate Oil Risk Near the Asset You Are Protecting

Every facility built around water — treatment plants, food production, marine vessels, wastewater infrastructure — faces an inherent contradiction when running oil hydraulics beside the water it protects. A water HPU removes that contradiction entirely.

⚡

Power Actuators, Valves, and Motors with Clean Hydraulic Energy

The HPU is the clean power source that drives the rest of the water hydraulic system. Actuators, gate valves, motors, and manifolds all receive steady, contamination-free hydraulic pressure from a single compact skid.

💰

Reduce Lifecycle Costs Through Lower Maintenance

No oil changes, no filter disposal, no hazardous waste management. PEEK wear surfaces extend rebuild intervals. Stainless construction eliminates corrosion-driven replacement. The lifecycle cost advantage compounds quickly.

📋

Ensure Compliance with Food Safety, Marine, and Environmental Regulations

Oil-free operation removes petroleum from regulated environments where any oil release requires reporting. WRAS-compliant tanks, ISO 9001 certification, and clean operation support audit readiness across all sectors.

🔮

Future-Proof Operations Against Tightening ESG Demands

Environmental regulations tighten every year. Specifying oil-free water hydraulics today removes the risk of forced retrofit later. The investment in clean hydraulics now is an investment in operational continuity tomorrow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Water Hydraulics HPU
Technical FAQ

WaterQuest Solutions water HPUs are available with 316 stainless steel reservoirs in four sizes: 5L (S05, for P1 pumps), 25L (S25, for P3 and P6), 55L (S55, for P6 and P15), and 90L (S90, for P15). All tanks conform to WRAS 1999 UK water regulations and include an overflow fitting as standard.

The WaterQuest Solutions Pick a Pack system builds your ordering code in five steps. Step 1 selects the pump and motor (P1, P3, P6, or P15 at your required pressure). Step 2 chooses the reservoir volume (5L to 90L in 316 SS). Step 3 selects the filter and protection package (1T, 2L, 3B, 4B, or X). Step 4 selects the control valve configuration (C01 to C07). Step 5 adds accessories such as hand pump (HP), gauges (SG), heat exchanger (HE), level gauge (LG), fan cooler (FC), or additional tank return (T). The codes combine into a single unit ordering reference.

Yes. WaterQuest water hydraulics HPUs deliver the same power output as traditional oil-driven packs but run 100% oil-free. The skid layout is designed for integration into existing facilities. Note that any actuators, valves, cylinders, and motors connected to the HPU must also be water-hydraulic rated. WaterQuest can supply or specify the complete oil-to-water circuit conversion.

The maximum continuous system pressure is 160 bar across all standard WaterQuest Solutions HPU configurations. The P1 pump is available up to 100 bar maximum in the HPU system, while P3, P6, and P15 are all available to the full 160 bar rating. P30, P60, and P180 larger systems are available on request.
