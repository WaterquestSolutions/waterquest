Materials Engineering

Why Every Material Choice Matters

Oil hydraulic systems are engineered around oil's properties: carbon steel, rubber seals, aluminium castings. Every one of those materials fails in water service. WaterQuest uses the right materials from the start.

🧱

316L Stainless Steel

Exceptional corrosion resistance in water, seawater, and chloride environments. Standard for all structural and wetted components throughout the WaterQuest range. The same alloy used in marine, pharmaceutical, and food processing infrastructure worldwide.

316L SS · EN 1.4404
⚗️

Carbon-Reinforced PEEK

Polyether ether ketone with carbon fibre reinforcement provides self-lubricating wear performance that water cannot deliver. Used for all bearing surfaces, piston seals, valve seats, and tribological contact points. Chemically inert to water, seawater, and most process fluids.

CF30-PEEK · Grade 450G
🔩

Ceramic and Hard Chrome Coatings

Piston rods in water hydraulic cylinders require hardness for seal wear resistance and corrosion protection that neither bare stainless nor chrome-plated carbon steel can reliably deliver. Ceramic and 17-4PH hard chrome coatings provide both in a single surface treatment.

Cr₂O₃ Ceramic · 17-4PH
🔬

PTFE and UHMWPE

Polytetrafluoroethylene and ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene complement PEEK in sealing applications where extreme chemical inertness or very low static friction is required. Used in cylinder seals, valve seats, and fittings throughout the WaterQuest range.

PTFE · UHMWPE
