Materials Engineering

Oil and water play by different rules. Good engineering respects the difference.

Oil and water can both transmit hydraulic power, but a system designed for oil cannot simply be expected to run reliably on water. Water demands a different approach to lubrication, wear and, especially, material compatibility.

Connected dissimilar metals in conductive water can cause galvanic corrosion. A small carbon steel fitting in a large stainless assembly is particularly vulnerable.

Select every wetted component for the water and its neighbouring materials. Our equipment uses 316 stainless steel and carbon-reinforced PEEK where suited to the duty. Specify compatible connection hardware throughout the water circuit. Coordinate hydraulic equipment and connection hardware through one WaterQuest contact.
