WaterQuest P30
Axial Piston Pump

The P30 is the high-flow industrial model in the WaterQuest axial piston range, delivering up to 66 L/min at a continuous 160 bar. Three displacement options of 23, 30, and 35 cc/rev sit within the same body envelope and mounting pattern, covering a wide range of motor sizes and flow requirements. Available in unboosted 2-port (PC/PA) and boosted 3-port (PB) configurations, plus a dedicated Power Pack Clockwise (PPC) assembly variant.

Why the P30

High-Flow Industrial Power.
Oil-Free, 316 Stainless.

The P30 nearly doubles the maximum flow of the P15, stepping from 37.2 L/min to 66 L/min within the same oil-free, 316 stainless steel package. With three displacement options and the introduction of 1" BSPP ports on the unboosted variants, the P30 is designed for high-flow industrial applications where port velocity and system pressure drop matter.

🔩

316 Stainless Steel

Full 316 SS construction throughout, corrosion-resistant in tap water, seawater, and light chemical service without coatings or surface treatment.

🔢

Three Displacement Options

23, 30, and 35 cc/rev share one body, one mounting pattern, and one port face. Displacement choice determines flow output and motor frame requirement without any piping changes.

💧

1" BSPP Ports (Unboosted)

The unboosted P30 introduces 1" BSPP on both the pressure and tank ports, correctly sized for flows up to 66 L/min with acceptable line velocity in connecting pipework.

🔄

Boosted and Unboosted

PC/PA 2-port unboosted for tank-fed HPU installations, PB 3-port boosted for elevated inlet supply and speeds above 1800 rpm, and PPC Power Pack variant for direct HPU assembly.

⚡

ATEX II 2GD Certified

Approved to Directive 94/9/EC for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust explosive atmospheres. T6 temperature class. Suitable for hazardous area installations across all three displacement variants.

☢️

F4N Fit For Nuclear

F4N accreditation confirms suitability for nuclear environments, eliminating oil fire risk and radiological contamination pathways from hydraulic circuits.

🌊

Seawater Compatible

Standard 316 SS construction handles seawater. Noble material internal packages available for extended offshore or subsea service. Specify fluid code S when ordering.

🔧

PPC Power Pack Option

Engineering Detail

P30 Design and
Construction

Significant Port Step-Up from P15

The most important engineering difference between the P30 and P15 is the port sizing on the unboosted configuration. The P15 unboosted uses G3/4" BSPP on both ports. The P30 unboosted steps up to 1" BSPP on both P and T ports, correctly sized for flow rates up to 66 L/min without excessive line velocity in connected pipework. The boosted PB configuration uses 3/4" BSPP pressure ports and 1/2" BSPP case drain, which is consistent with the boosted P15.

Larger Body and Shaft

The P30 body outer diameter grows to 150 mm from 114 mm on the P15. The shaft increases to 23.993/23.980 mm with an 8x7 mm key, compared to 18.99/18.97 mm and 6x6 mm on the P15. The port face is a 110 mm square arrangement with 32 mm spacing. All three P30 displacement variants share these identical external dimensions, simplifying system design and allowing displacement changes without modifying the piping or bell housing.

PPC Power Pack Variant

The PPC (Power Pack Clockwise) variant is unique to the P30 family and is shown on the unboosted drawing. It adds an O-ring face seal on the port face and M8 mounting holes on a 116 mm PCD. These features allow the pump to be bolted directly into a power pack assembly without the need for separate flanges or adaptor plates. The PPC option applies only to the clockwise unboosted variant; PA (anti-clockwise) and PB (boosted) do not have this option.

Supply Pipework Sizing

At P30 flow rates the supply pipework bore is especially important on the unboosted configuration. A 66 L/min flow rate through an undersized supply pipe creates significant inlet pressure drop, which can cause the pump inlet to go negative and result in cavitation damage within minutes. Size all supply piping to ensure inlet flow is at least 20% above maximum pump delivery at the lowest tank level. The 1" BSPP tank port on the unboosted P30 is the minimum acceptable bore for the connecting pipe; larger bore is preferred on any run exceeding 500 mm.

Shaft Coupling Requirements

The P30 connects to an electric motor via a bell housing and coupling. At the power levels required for the 35cc variant at full pressure (up to 19.5 kW), motor frame sizing and coupling alignment are critical. The pump cannot accept induced axial or radial shaft loads. Always use a 3-part gear coupling unless shaft concentricity between motor and pump is within 0.05 mm, in which case direct inline connection is permissible. Motor frame sizes range from 100L through 180M depending on displacement and operating pressure.

Temperature and Fluid Compatibility

Full rated performance from 2°C to 50°C. Above 50°C volumetric efficiency reduces; consult WaterQuest for high-temperature applications. Monopropylene Glycol antifreeze available for sub-zero operation. The pump operates on glycol 95/5 mixtures with internal clearance adjustment. For seawater service, noble material internal packages are required. The exact operating fluid must be specified on all enquiries and orders; fluid chemistry affects seal material selection and noble material requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

P30 Pump
Technical FAQ

The unboosted P30 (PC/PA/PPC) uses 1" BSPP on both the pressure and tank ports, correctly sized for flows up to 66 L/min. The boosted PB uses 3/4" BSPP on the pressure outlet and 1/2" BSPP on the case drain T port. The case drain T port on the boosted variant carries only internal leakage flow, which is a small fraction of total pump output, so a smaller port is adequate and correct. The pressure outlet on the PB is 3/4" BSPP because the boosted body design accommodates the two pressure port arrangement differently from the single large unboosted pressure port. Always verify which configuration you are ordering before selecting pipework, fittings, and manifold connections.

The PPC (Power Pack Clockwise) is the clockwise unboosted variant with an O-ring face seal on the port face and M8 mounting holes on a 116 mm PCD added to the body. These additions allow the pump to be bolted directly onto a manifold plate or reservoir top plate without separate adaptor flanges, simplifying HPU assembly and reducing potential leak paths. Specify PPC when you are building a compact HPU where direct port-to-manifold connection is preferred over separate pipework. The PPC is only available in the clockwise direction; anti-clockwise power pack builds use the PA variant with a separate adaptor plate.

The P30 at 35cc and 1500 rpm reaches a peak overall efficiency of approximately 87% at 80 bar, which is the highest peak efficiency in the compact and mid-range series. The 1500 rpm curve maintains efficiency above 84% from 60 bar through to 120 bar, making the P30 particularly well suited to sustained mid-pressure operation. The 1000 rpm curve peaks at around 84% at 80 bar and holds above 80% through to 100 bar before declining. At 500 rpm efficiency drops sharply above 80 bar, confirming this is a low-speed startup or positioning speed only, not a sustained operating point at high pressure.

The P30 body outer diameter is 150 mm compared to 114 mm on the P15, an increase of 36 mm. The shaft grows from 18.99/18.97 mm with a 6x6 mm key to 23.993/23.980 mm with an 8x7 mm key. The overall body length increases from 145 mm to 156 mm. The port face is a 110 mm square arrangement on the P30 vs the round port face of the P15. These dimensional changes reflect the significant jump in power handling, from 11 kW maximum on the P15 to 19.5 kW on the P30. Bell housing and coupling specifications must be checked carefully when transitioning between P15 and P30 in an existing installation.

No. The maximum shaft speed for the unboosted PC, PA, and PPC configurations is 1800 rpm. Running above 1800 rpm on an unboosted configuration risks cavitation inside the pump casing and will cause accelerated internal wear and seal failure. To achieve shaft speeds above 1800 rpm or to reach the full rated flow of 66 L/min, the boosted PB configuration must be specified with a minimum inlet supply pressure of 2.5 bar maintained at all times. If a VFD is used for speed control, set the maximum frequency limit to the value that corresponds to 1800 rpm for the motor fitted before first commissioning.
