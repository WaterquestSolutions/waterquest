Why the P30

High-Flow Industrial Power. Oil-Free, 316 Stainless.

The P30 offers displacement and inlet choices for a larger flow requirement. Review output at your operating speed and pressure, then size the connections and pipework to support that flow with appropriate inlet conditions and manageable losses.

316 Stainless Steel

Specify water chemistry and temperature for material and seal selection. Seawater requires an application-specific configuration.

Three Displacement Options

23, 30 and 35 cc/rev (1.40, 1.83 and 2.14 in³/rev) share one body, one mounting pattern, and one port face. Displacement choice determines flow output and motor frame requirement without any piping changes.

1" BSPP Ports (Unboosted)

The unboosted P30 introduces 1" BSPP on both the pressure and tank ports, correctly sized for flows up to 66 L/min (17.4 US gpm) with acceptable line velocity in connecting pipework.

Boosted and Unboosted

PC/PA 2-port unboosted for tank-fed HPU installations, PB 3-port boosted for elevated inlet supply and speeds above 1800 rpm, and PPC Power Pack variant for direct HPU assembly.

Hazardous-area documentation

For hazardous-area duty, request current conformity documents for the exact model, configuration and intended zone. Include the installation requirements with your enquiry.

PPC Power Pack Option

The PPC variant adds an O-ring face seal and M8 mounting holes on a 116 mm (4.57 in) PCD, simplifying direct HPU integration without separate adaptor flanges or manifold plates.

Engineering Detail

P30 Design and Construction

Significant Port Step-Up from P15

The most important engineering difference between the P30 and P15 is the port sizing on the unboosted configuration. The P15 unboosted uses G3/4" BSPP on both ports. The P30 unboosted steps up to 1" BSPP on both P and T ports, correctly sized for flow rates up to 66 L/min (17.4 US gpm) without excessive line velocity in connected pipework. The boosted PB configuration uses 3/4" BSPP pressure ports and 1/2" BSPP case drain, which is consistent with the boosted P15.

Larger Body and Shaft

The P30 body outer diameter grows to 150 mm (5.91 in) from 114 mm (4.49 in) on the P15. The shaft increases to 23.993/23.980 mm (0.945/0.944 in) with an 8x7 mm (0.315x0.276 in) key, compared to 18.99/18.97 mm (0.748/0.747 in) and 6x6 mm (0.236x0.236 in) on the P15. The port face is a 110 mm (4.33 in) square arrangement with 32 mm (1.26 in) spacing. All three P30 displacement variants share these identical external dimensions, simplifying system design and allowing displacement changes without modifying the piping or bell housing.

PPC Power Pack Variant

The PPC (Power Pack Clockwise) variant is unique to the P30 family and is shown on the unboosted drawing. It adds an O-ring face seal on the port face and M8 mounting holes on a 116 mm (4.57 in) PCD. The PPC option applies only to the clockwise unboosted variant; PA (anti-clockwise) and PB (boosted) do not have this option.

Supply Pipework Sizing

At P30 flow rates the supply pipework bore is especially important on the unboosted configuration. A 66 L/min (17.4 US gpm) flow rate through an undersized supply pipe creates significant inlet pressure drop, which can cause the pump inlet to go negative and result in cavitation damage within minutes. Size all supply piping to ensure inlet flow is at least 20% above maximum pump delivery at the lowest tank level. The 1" BSPP tank port on the unboosted P30 is the minimum acceptable bore for the connecting pipe; larger bore is preferred on any run exceeding 500 mm.

Shaft Coupling Requirements

The P30 connects to an electric motor via a bell housing and coupling. At the power levels required for the 35cc (2.14 in³) variant at full pressure (up to 19.5 kW (26.1 hp)), motor frame sizing and coupling alignment are critical. The pump cannot accept induced axial or radial shaft loads. Always use a 3-part gear coupling unless shaft concentricity between motor and pump is within 0.05 mm (0.00197 in), in which case direct inline connection is permissible. Motor frame sizes range from 100L through 180M depending on displacement and operating pressure.

Frequently Asked Questions

P30 Pump Technical FAQ

The unboosted P30 (PC/PA/PPC) uses 1" BSPP on both the pressure and tank ports, correctly sized for flows up to 66 L/min (17.4 US gpm). The boosted PB uses 3/4" BSPP on the pressure outlet and 1/2" BSPP on the case drain T port. The pressure outlet on the PB is 3/4" BSPP because the boosted body design accommodates the two pressure port arrangement differently from the single large unboosted pressure port.

The PPC (Power Pack Clockwise) is the clockwise unboosted variant with an O-ring face seal on the port face and M8 mounting holes on a 116 mm (4.57 in) PCD added to the body.
