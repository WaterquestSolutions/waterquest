P1 at a glance

What it is

A Janus axial-piston pump designed for a compact water-hydraulic duty. It is not an external gear pump.

Displacement

Manufacturer-listed options: 0.8 and 1.2 cm³/rev (0.0488 and 0.0732 in³/rev). Displacement and shaft speed define theoretical flow before leakage.

Selection status

Choose the exact rotation, seal, fluid and duty with the manufacturer’s configuration record. Published pressure information is inconsistent; do not use a blanket 160 bar rating.

A pressure limit must belong to the exact order

Use the manufacturer-issued configuration record to establish the P1 continuous pressure, speed and temperature limits. The quotation must identify the applicable rating and duty-point curve.
