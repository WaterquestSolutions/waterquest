Engineering Design

Built From First Principles.
Not Adapted From Oil.

The P1 is not an oil hydraulic pump with water bolted on. Every internal is purpose-built for water as the working fluid. CF30-PEEK (carbon fibre reinforced PEEK) components resist corrosion and wear in fresh or salt water environments without the lubrication oil-based systems depend on. The result is a pump that is genuinely oil-free from drive shaft to port face.

316L Stainless Steel Housing

Full 316L stainless construction handles fresh water, sea water, brackish water, and light chemical solutions without corrosion or surface degradation. Meets food-grade and marine specifications out of the box.

CF30-PEEK Internal Components

Carbon fibre reinforced PEEK provides exceptional wear resistance and dimensional stability without oil lubrication. Self-lubricating behaviour in water eliminates the need for any external fluid additives or scheduled re-greasing intervals.

Zero Contamination Risk

No oil in the system means no risk of hydraulic fluid entering the process, the environment, or the water table. Fully compliant with Fisheries Act requirements and CleanBC objectives for industrial hydraulic circuits near waterways.

Compact Footprint

Miniature gear pump geometry in a package small enough for embedded HPU installations and tight enclosures. The P1 fits where larger pump lines simply cannot, making it ideal for mobile, subsea, and lab-scale systems.

Two Displacement Options

Choose 0.8 cc/rev for precision low-flow work or 1.2 cc/rev where higher throughput is required. Both share identical mounting geometry, allowing in-field displacement changes without repiping. Speed ranges from 500 to 1750 rpm.

Low Maintenance Lifecycle

Water hydraulics eliminate scheduled oil changes, filter replacements, and oil disposal costs that add up over the life of a system. The P1 is designed for long-interval service in demanding industrial environments.

Applications

Where the P1 Excels

The P1 is sized for applications where compact form factor, zero oil contamination, and reliable low-flow delivery matter more than raw volume output. Anywhere traditional oil hydraulics creates a contamination or environmental liability, the P1 is the engineering answer.

🔬

Laboratory and Research Systems

Ideal for bench-scale hydraulic test rigs and R&D circuits where clean operation and repeatable performance are critical. Zero contamination from hydraulic fluid ensures test data accuracy.

🌊

Marine and Subsea Applications

316L stainless and PEEK construction tolerates full seawater exposure. Eliminates the catastrophic environmental and regulatory risk of oil leaks on vessels and offshore platforms.

💧

Water Treatment Infrastructure

Used in actuator circuits, valve pilots, and positioning systems at wastewater treatment plants where oil ingress into process streams is a permit and Fisheries Act violation risk.

🏭

Food and Beverage Processing

Food-safe 316L stainless construction and a water working fluid make the P1 compliant for use in food handling, brewing, and pharmaceutical manufacturing environments.

⚡

Green Hydrogen and Electrolysis

Supports water management and pressure control circuits in hydrogen electrolyser plants where any oil contamination would damage the membrane stack and void warranties.

🔧

OEM HPU Integration

The P1 pairs directly into WaterQuest HPU packages. Compact dimensions allow embedded installation inside skid-mounted power units for full plug-and-play deployment on any job site.

HPU Integration

The P1 Inside a WaterQuest HPU

The P1 is the heart of WaterQuest's compact HPU offerings. Paired with an electric motor, reservoir, filtration block, and manifold, it ships as a complete hydraulic power unit ready for connection. This is the starting point for customers who want a full turnkey water hydraulic system without engineering a custom circuit from scratch.

For larger applications requiring more flow or redundancy, multiple P1 units can be run in parallel within a single HPU enclosure. This modularity also provides graceful degradation; if one pump requires service, the circuit stays operational.

Explore WaterQuest HPU Packages

P1 HPU Configuration Options

The following configurations are available when ordering the P1 as part of a WaterQuest HPU package. All units use tap water or softened seawater as the working fluid with no additives required.

  • Single P1 with TEFC motor, 1 litre reservoir and bypass relief valve
  • Dual P1 parallel configuration for 4.2 L/min maximum throughput
  • Integrated pressure transducer and 4-20mA output for SCADA connection
  • Variable frequency drive (VFD) coupling for proportional flow control
  • 316L stainless manifold block with up to 4 work ports
  • Optional inline filtration to 5 micron for high-pressure circuits
  • NEMA 4X or IP67 enclosure for outdoor and marine environments
