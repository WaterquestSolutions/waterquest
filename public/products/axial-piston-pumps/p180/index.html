Why the P180

430 L/min.
The Top of the Range.

The P180 is a different class of pump from everything below it. It is supplied exclusively in the boosted configuration with a mechanical seal, uses a 13-tooth spline drive shaft instead of a keyed connection, and employs 2" SAE 3000 PSI 4-bolt flanged pressure ports. These are not incremental changes; they reflect the engineering demands of sustained 430 L/min oil-free water hydraulic flow at 160 bar.

💧

430 L/min at 160 Bar

Maximum water hydraulic flow in the WaterQuest range. At 1750 rpm the 225cc variant delivers approximately 385 L/min at 20 bar. At 2000 rpm boosted the full 430 L/min is achieved.

⛓️

Spline Drive Shaft

13-tooth, 8/16 pitch, fillet root, side fit spline. No key. The spline drive distributes input torque evenly across all teeth, handling the 600 Nm maximum input torque at 160 bar without key stress concentration.

🔧

2" SAE 4-Bolt Flange Ports

Pressure ports use 2" SAE 3000 PSI 4-bolt flanged connections, not BSPP threads. Flanged connections are required at this pressure and flow combination to maintain joint integrity and allow assembly without large torque wrenches.

🛡️

Mechanical Seal Standard

The P180 is supplied exclusively with a mechanical seal; no lip seal option is available. At these shaft speeds and fluid pressures, the mechanical seal provides superior service life and eliminates the periodic replacement required by a lip seal.

🏭

Boosted Only Configuration

The P180 is exclusively available in the 3-port boosted (PB) configuration. A minimum 2.5 bar inlet supply is required at all times. There is no unboosted PC, PA, or PPC variant for the P180.

🔩

316 Stainless Steel

Full 316 SS construction. Corrosion-resistant in tap water, seawater, and light chemical environments. Noble material internal packages available for sustained seawater service. Specify fluid code S on enquiry.

🏭

ATEX II 2GD Certified

Approved to Directive 94/9/EC for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust explosive atmospheres. T6 temperature class. Applies across all three P180 displacement variants.

☢️

F4N Fit For Nuclear

F4N accreditation confirms suitability for nuclear environments. The P180 eliminates both the fire risk and radiological contamination pathways that come with oil-based hydraulics in safety-critical applications.

Engineering Detail

P180 Design and
Construction

Spline Drive: Why It Matters

At 225cc and 160 bar, the P180 requires approximately 605 Nm of input torque. A single keyway concentrates this torque at one point on the shaft, creating high localised stress that can cause shaft and coupling fatigue over time. The 13-tooth 8/16 pitch spline distributes that same torque evenly across all 13 teeth, dramatically reducing stress concentration. This is not an unusual design for pumps at this power level; it is standard engineering practice. Any coupling or gearbox interface connected to the P180 must use a matching 13-tooth 8/16 pitch spline sleeve. Confirm pitch and tooth count before ordering or manufacturing a coupling.

2" SAE Flange: Not BSPP

The pressure ports on the P180 use 2" SAE 3000 PSI 4-bolt flanged connections. This is the first and only pump in the WaterQuest range to use SAE flanged ports; all smaller models use BSPP threaded ports. SAE flanged connections are the industry standard for high-pressure, high-flow hydraulic circuits where threaded port integrity is difficult to maintain over time and maintenance cycles. All connecting pressure pipes, valves, cylinders, and manifolds in a P180 circuit must use matching 2" SAE 3000 PSI flanged connections. Do not use BSPP-to-SAE adaptors in the main pressure circuit at rated pressure; the adaptor becomes a stress concentration and potential leak path.

Mechanical Seal: Maintenance Implications

The P180 is supplied exclusively with a mechanical seal as standard. There is no lip seal option. The mechanical seal provides superior service life at these shaft speeds and fluid pressures, but it is not field-replaceable on a running pump. Seal maintenance requires pump removal and disassembly. Plan seal replacement as a scheduled maintenance event, not a reactive repair. Consult WaterQuest for seal service intervals based on your specific operating conditions and duty cycle, and confirm the seal kit part number at the time of initial commissioning.

Body Size and Weight

At 252 mm outer diameter and 389.5 mm overall length, the P180 is a substantial piece of equipment. A tapped M12 hole on the port face accepts a lifting eye for safe handling. Always use the lifting eye when moving the pump. The pump must not be lifted by the shaft, port connections, or drive flange. Ensure that the support structure, skid, and bell housing are designed for the combined weight of pump and motor, including dynamic loads during startup and operation.

Inlet Supply Requirements

The P180 requires a minimum 2.5 bar inlet pressure at all times on the water inlet port. There is no unboosted configuration option. At 430 L/min maximum flow, even a 0.5 bar drop below the 2.5 bar minimum at the pump inlet during high-demand transients can cause cavitation. Install a pressure gauge and low-pressure cut-off switch directly on the pump inlet to protect against inlet pressure loss. The inlet supply can come from a high-pressure mains connection, a booster pump on a reservoir system, or a water column above 25 metres. Consult WaterQuest to confirm the boost pump sizing for your specific reservoir and pipework layout.

Temperature and Fluid Compatibility

Full rated performance from 2°C to 50°C. Above 50°C volumetric efficiency reduces; consult WaterQuest for specific high-temperature applications. Monopropylene Glycol antifreeze available for sub-zero operation. The pump operates on glycol 95/5 mixtures with internal clearance adjustment. For seawater service, noble material internal packages are required; specify fluid code S on all enquiries and orders. The exact operating fluid must be confirmed at the time of ordering without exception; fluid chemistry affects seal selection and internal clearances.

Frequently Asked Questions

P180 Pump
Technical FAQ

At 225cc displacement and 2000 rpm, the P180 is moving up to 430 L/min of water through the pump. Supplying this volume from a gravity-fed tank without positive inlet pressure is not reliably possible without creating a vacuum at the pump inlet during high-demand transients. The boosted configuration, requiring a minimum 2.5 bar inlet supply at all times, is the only way to ensure cavitation-free operation at these flow rates and shaft speeds. This is not a design limitation; it is sound hydraulic engineering. Every P180 installation requires a confirmed 2.5 bar or greater inlet supply from mains pressure, a boost pump, or a sufficient water column before the order should be placed.

The P180 spline shaft is a 13-tooth, 8/16 pitch (diametral pitch), fillet root, side fit spline. This is the same spline standard used in large hydraulic pump drives and power take-off applications globally. The connecting coupling or motor shaft sleeve must be machined or selected to match this specification exactly. Do not attempt to use a keyed adaptor or a coupling designed for a different spline pitch. Consult WaterQuest at the enquiry stage for guidance on coupling selection and bell housing design, since the coupling must also accommodate the SAE B or C flange motor mounting and ensure zero induced axial or radial load on the pump shaft.

SAE 4-bolt flanged connections use a flat face seal gasket and four bolts to make the connection, rather than a tapered thread. For 2" SAE 3000 PSI (code 61 or code 62 flange per SAE J518) the bolt circle diameter and bolt size are standardised. All connecting pipes, valves, manifolds, and cylinders in the P180 pressure circuit must use matching 2" SAE flanged ends. This means flanged hose assemblies, flanged block valves, and flanged pipe fittings throughout. Standard BSPP fittings and adaptors are not acceptable in the main pressure circuit of a P180 installation at rated pressure. BSPP connections may be used on low-pressure parts of the circuit only.

The P180 at 225cc and 1500 rpm achieves a peak overall efficiency of approximately 90% at 80 to 120 bar. This is the highest sustained efficiency in the WaterQuest axial piston range, maintaining above 87% from 60 bar through to 160 bar. At 1000 rpm the peak is approximately 88% at 80 bar. At 500 rpm the peak of approximately 85% occurs at 80 bar, with a moderate decline above that. Across the mid-pressure range from 60 to 140 bar, all three speed curves maintain above 84% efficiency, confirming the P180 as the most efficient pump in the range at sustained high-pressure duty.

The P180 body outer diameter is 252 mm compared to 200 mm on the P60, a 52 mm increase. The spigot grows from 127.00/126.95 mm on the P60 to 152.35/152.30 mm on the P180. The overall length increases from 199 mm on the P60 to 389.5 mm on the P180, a significant growth that reflects the much larger displacement. The drive changes from a keyed 27.99 mm shaft on the P60 to a 13-tooth spline on the P180. The port face expands from 139 mm square on the P60 to 193 mm square on the P180. And critically, the pressure ports change from 1" BSPP on the P60 to 2" SAE flanged on the P180. These differences mean P60 and P180 are not in any way dimensionally compatible; a system designed around the P60 requires complete redesign of the bell housing, coupling, pipework, and manifold connections before accepting a P180.
