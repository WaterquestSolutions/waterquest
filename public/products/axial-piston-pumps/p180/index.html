Why the P180

430 L/min (114 US gpm). The Top of the Range.

The P180 brings higher-flow pumping into a package with a boosted inlet, mechanical seal, spline drive and flanged pressure connections. Select the inlet, drive and pipework together, using the stated performance curves and installation requirements for the proposed operating point.

430 L/min (114 US gpm) at 160 Bar

Maximum water hydraulic flow in the WaterQuest range. At 1750 rpm the 225cc (13.7 in³) variant delivers approximately 385 L/min (102 US gpm) at 20 bar (290 psi). At 2000 rpm boosted the full 430 L/min (114 US gpm) is achieved.

Spline Drive Shaft

13-tooth, 8/16 pitch, fillet root, side fit spline. The spline drive distributes input torque evenly across all teeth, handling the 600 Nm (443 lbf·ft) maximum input torque at 160 bar (2,321 psi) without key stress concentration.

2" (50.8 mm) SAE 4-Bolt Flange Ports

Pressure ports use 2" (50.8 mm) SAE Code 61 4-bolt flanged connections, not BSPP threads. Flanged connections are required at this pressure and flow combination to maintain joint integrity and allow assembly without large torque wrenches.

Mechanical Seal Standard

Use the performance data and service instructions for the exact model at the intended duty. Efficiency, wear and replacement intervals depend on operating conditions and the selected component construction.

Boosted Only Configuration

The P180 is exclusively available in the 3-port boosted (PB) configuration. A minimum 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) inlet supply is required at all times. There is no unboosted PC, PA, or PPC variant for the P180.

316 Stainless Steel

Full 316 SS construction. Corrosion-resistant in tap water, seawater, and light chemical environments. Noble material internal packages available for sustained seawater service. Specify fluid code S on enquiry.

Hazardous-area documentation

For hazardous-area duty, request current conformity documents for the exact model, configuration and intended zone. Include the installation requirements with your enquiry.

Boosted Only. P180 Installation Requirements

The P180 is only supplied in the 3-port boosted configuration with a mechanical seal. Every P180 order requires a 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) minimum inlet supply pressure maintained at all times.

3-Port Boosted — Mechanical Seal — All Displacements

PB160-135W / PB160-180W / PB160-225W

All P180 orders use this configuration. Minimum 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) inlet supply pressure is mandatory. The pump is bi-directional, with inlet and outlet ports reversing with shaft rotation direction.

The P180 is exclusively supplied in the 3-port boosted configuration. No unboosted 2-port (PC/PA) or PPC variant exists at this pump size. Every installation must provide a minimum 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) inlet supply pressure at all times.
Three ports: pressure outlet (P), water inlet, and a separate case drain port (T). Internal leakage is routed through the 1" BSPP T port to a low-pressure sump or drain. Case drain pressure must not exceed 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) at any operating condition.
Pressure ports use 2" (50.8 mm) SAE Code 61 4-bolt flanged connections, not BSPP threads. All connecting pressure pipework, valves, and manifolds must use matching 2" (50.8 mm) SAE Code 61 flanged connections. Do not use BSPP-to-SAE adaptors in the main pressure circuit at rated pressure.
The shaft uses a 13-tooth 8/16 pitch spline, not a keyed connection. The coupling or drive sleeve must be designed for this spline specification. Confirm tooth count, pitch, and fit class before manufacturing any coupling.
Mechanical seal as standard throughout. The seal is not field-replaceable on a running pump; plan seal replacement into scheduled maintenance intervals. Consult WaterQuest for seal change procedures at first installation.
The pump is bi-directional. Inlet and outlet pressure ports change with shaft rotation direction. Clearly label shaft rotation direction on installation drawings and commission with a rotation check before pressurizing the circuit.
M12 eye bolt tapped hole is provided on the port face for pump handling. Use an appropriate M12 rated lifting eye at all times when moving the pump. Do not attempt to lift by the shaft or port connections.
Maximum shaft speed: 2000 rpm. Minimum shaft speed for sustained high-pressure operation: 1000 rpm. Efficiency above 500 rpm is acceptable for low-speed positioning sequences.

Engineering Detail

P180 Design and Construction

Spline Drive: Why It Matters

At 225cc (13.7 in³) and 160 bar (2,321 psi), the P180 requires approximately 605 Nm (446 lbf·ft) of input torque. A single keyway concentrates this torque at one point on the shaft, creating high localized stress that can cause shaft and coupling fatigue over time. The 13-tooth 8/16 pitch spline distributes that same torque evenly across all 13 teeth, dramatically reducing stress concentration. This is not an unusual design for pumps at this power level; it is standard engineering practice. Any coupling or gearbox interface connected to the P180 must use a matching 13-tooth 8/16 pitch spline sleeve.

2" (50.8 mm) SAE Flange: Not BSPP

The P180 pressure ports use 2" (50.8 mm) SAE Code 61 4-bolt flanged connections. Match the mating flange dimensions, pressure rating, seal and fasteners to the pump drawing. Size the rest of the circuit for its flow, pressure loss and component requirements; any transition to another connection standard needs a suitable rated fitting and a supported installation.

Mechanical Seal: Maintenance Implications

The P180 is supplied exclusively with a mechanical seal as standard. There is no lip seal option. Plan seal replacement as a scheduled maintenance event, not a reactive repair.

Body Size and Weight

At 252 mm (9.92 in) outer diameter and 389.5 mm (15.3 in) overall length, the P180 is a substantial piece of equipment. A tapped M12 hole on the port face accepts a lifting eye for safe handling. The pump must not be lifted by the shaft, port connections, or drive flange.

Inlet Supply Requirements

The P180 requires a minimum 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) inlet pressure at all times on the water inlet port. There is no unboosted configuration option. At 430 L/min (114 US gpm) maximum flow, even a 0.5 bar (7.25 psi) drop below the 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) minimum at the pump inlet during high-demand transients can cause cavitation. The inlet supply can come from a high-pressure mains connection, a booster pump on a reservoir system, or a water column above 25 metres (82 ft).

How to Order

P180 Ordering Code Structure

The P180 uses PB (Pump Boosted) as the only unit type. All P180 orders are mechanical seal as standard. Specify displacement and fluid code. All three displacements use the same code structure.

Example Ordering Code

PB160-225W

Boosted P180, 160 bar (2,321 psi), 225 cc/rev (13.7 in³/rev), tap water, mechanical seal standard. For 180cc (11 in³): PB160-180W. For 135cc (8.24 in³): PB160-135W. For seawater: PB160-225S.

Note: No MS (mechanical seal) suffix is needed on P180 orders. Mechanical seal is the standard and only option. The pump is supplied exclusively as PB; there is no PC, PA, or PPC variant.

Before you order: The P180 is a large industrial pump requiring specialized motor coupling (spline drive), SAE flanged pipework, and a confirmed 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) inlet supply. WaterQuest recommends a technical review of every P180 application before order placement to confirm motor frame, coupling design, and pipework sizing.

Frequently Asked Questions

P180 Pump Technical FAQ

At 225cc (13.7 in³) and 2000 rpm, the listed maximum flow is 430 L/min (114 US gpm).

The P180 spline shaft is a 13-tooth, 8/16 pitch (diametral pitch), fillet root, side fit spline. This is the same spline standard used in large hydraulic pump drives and power take-off applications globally. The connecting coupling or motor shaft sleeve must be machined or selected to match this specification exactly. Do not attempt to use a keyed adaptor or a coupling designed for a different spline pitch. Consult WaterQuest at the enquiry stage for guidance on coupling selection and bell housing design, since the coupling must also accommodate the SAE B or C flange motor mounting and ensure zero induced axial or radial load on the pump shaft.
