Inlet Configuration

2-Port and 3-Port
Inlet Conditions

All P and T ports are clearly marked. Never reverse port connections. BSPP parallel thread with high-pressure sealing washers only.

2-Port

Unboosted Configuration

Low-pressure supply from local tank or water mains
Inlet pressure must remain positive at all times
Cannot self-prime. Flooded suction required.
Maximum inlet supply pressure 2.5 bar
Inlet flow must exceed delivery flow by 20% minimum
P and T ports clearly marked. Never reverse.
3-Port

Boosted Configuration

Supply pressure 2.5 bar or above (mains, 25 m+ column, or boost pump)
Separates pump inlet from internal leakage water
Leakage must drain to low-pressure sump
Minimum inlet 2.0 bar above T-port pressure at all times
Maximum casing pressure 2.5 bar
Can rotate in either direction. Ports auto-assign.

Fluid Compatibility

Four Fluid Categories.
Select on Order.

W

Tap Water

EEC Directive 98/83/EC standard potable water. Standard internal material selection. No additives required.

S

Sea Water

Noble material construction advised. Specify seawater grade on order for appropriate internal material selection across all wetted components.

T

Technical Water

Distilled, RO-treated, or demineralised water. Suitable for pharmaceutical, nuclear, and precision industrial processes requiring ultra-pure water circuits.

O

Other Fluids

Glycol 95/5 mixtures and specialist process fluids. Specify fluid composition on enquiry so the correct internal material package can be selected.

WaterQuest Solutions Pump
Technical FAQ

The WaterQuest Solutions P180 delivers a maximum water flow of 430 l/min at a maximum displacement of 225 cc/rev and maximum input power of 114 kW. It requires boosted inlet operation at up to 2000 RPM and is rated for a continuous operating pressure of 160 bar. It is the largest model in the WaterQuest Solutions range and is suited to major industrial, offshore, and infrastructure applications.

Yes. WaterQuest Solutions axial piston pumps are compatible with seawater when noble material construction is specified. Select fluid code S when ordering and the appropriate internal material package will be selected for saline environments. This includes marine deck equipment, offshore platforms, and coastal industrial applications.

The 2-port (unboosted) configuration is used with low-pressure supply from a local tank or water mains up to 2.5 bar. The 3-port (boosted) configuration is used when inlet supply pressure is 2.5 bar or above and separates the pump inlet from internal leakage water, enabling higher shaft speeds and allowing the P180 to operate at all. The 3-port design requires leakage to drain to a low-pressure sump and minimum inlet of 2.0 bar above T-port pressure at all times.

Variable displacement is available on the P60 and P180 only. Fixed displacement models (P1 through P30) are available in clockwise (PC), anti-clockwise (PA), and boosted (PB) configurations. For applications requiring variable flow control, the P60 or P180 should be specified with the PV unit type in the ordering code.
