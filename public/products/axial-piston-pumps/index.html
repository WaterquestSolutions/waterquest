Inlet Configuration

2-Port and 3-Port Inlet Conditions

All P and T ports are clearly marked. Never reverse port connections. BSPP parallel thread with high-pressure sealing washers only.

2-Port

Unboosted Configuration

Low-pressure supply from local tank or water mains
Inlet pressure must remain positive at all times
Cannot self-prime. Flooded suction required.
Maximum inlet supply pressure 2.5 bar (36.3 psi)
Inlet flow must exceed delivery flow by 20% minimum
P and T ports clearly marked. Never reverse.
3-Port

Boosted Configuration

Supply pressure 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) or above (mains, 25 m (82 ft)+ column, or boost pump)
Separates pump inlet from internal leakage water
Leakage must drain to low-pressure sump
Minimum inlet 2.0 bar (29 psi) above T-port pressure at all times
Maximum casing pressure 2.5 bar (36.3 psi)
Can rotate in either direction. Ports auto-assign.

Fluid Compatibility

Four Fluid Categories. Select on Order.

W

Tap Water

EEC Directive (EU) 2020/2184 standard potable water. Standard internal material selection. No additives required.

S

Sea Water

Noble material construction advised. Specify seawater grade on order for appropriate internal material selection across all wetted components.

T

Technical Water

Distilled, RO-treated, or demineralized water. Suitable for pharmaceutical, nuclear, and precision industrial processes requiring ultra-pure water circuits.

O

Other Fluids

Glycol 95/5 mixtures and specialist process fluids. Specify fluid composition on enquiry so the correct internal material package can be selected.

Key Applications

Where Pumps Are Specified

Food and Beverage

Water-powered actuation for handling and processing equipment where avoiding hydraulic oil near ingredients, packaging and washdown areas is a priority.

Offshore and Marine

Pump supplies for deck equipment, valves and other hydraulic mechanisms. Match each model to the specified water source, corrosion exposure and operating duty.

Fire and Emergency

Pump supplies for compatible water-mist systems and emergency mechanisms, selected against the complete system’s specified pressure and flow requirements.

Water Jetting

Water supply for cleaning, rinsing and suitable jetting duties. Match the pump, nozzle and filtration to the required pressure, flow and water quality.

Chemical and Pharma

Actuation for process valves and handling equipment where hydraulic-oil leakage could affect products or work areas. Review fluid and material compatibility.

Potable Water

Gate, valve and equipment actuation around treatment and distribution infrastructure where reducing hydraulic-oil leakage exposure is a design priority.

Industrial Automation

Hydraulic power for cylinders, motors and controlled mechanisms. Match pump output to load, movement speed, duty cycle and the control system.

Pump Technical FAQ

The P180 delivers a maximum water flow of 430 l/min (114 US gpm) at a maximum displacement of 225 cc/rev (13.7 in³/rev) and maximum input power of 114 kW (153 hp). It requires boosted inlet operation at up to 2000 RPM and is rated for a continuous operating pressure of 160 bar (2,321 psi).

Yes. Axial piston pumps are compatible with seawater when noble material construction is specified.

The 2-port (unboosted) configuration is used with low-pressure supply from a local tank or water mains up to 2.5 bar (36.3 psi). The 3-port (boosted) configuration is used when inlet supply pressure is 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) or above and separates the pump inlet from internal leakage water, enabling higher shaft speeds and allowing the P180 to operate at all. The 3-port design requires leakage to drain to a low-pressure sump and minimum inlet of 2.0 bar (29 psi) above T-port pressure at all times.

Variable displacement is available on the P60 and P180 only. Do not infer a boosted or anti-clockwise P1 option from another pump in the range. For applications requiring variable flow control, the P60 or P180 should be specified with the PV unit type in the ordering code.

Unboosted and boosted inlet arrangements

An unboosted configuration draws from its specified tank or inlet supply. A boosted configuration requires a positive inlet supply at the pump under operating flow, with the appropriate inlet protection and case-drain arrangement. The required pressure, allowable speed and port connections belong to the selected model and configuration.

Use the model’s specification and current installation drawing. Do not transfer one model’s inlet-pressure requirement or port function to another configuration.
