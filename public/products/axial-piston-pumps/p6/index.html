It steps up from the P3 with two displacement options of 4.6 and 6.0 cc/rev, and is available in unboosted 2-port and boosted 3-port configurations for maximum installation flexibility.

Why the P6

Double the Displacement Range.
Double the Flexibility.

The P6 is the first model in the range to offer two selectable displacement options in the same body. The 4.6 cc/rev variant delivers lower flow with a reduced motor drive requirement; the 6.0 cc/rev variant pushes flow to 12 L/min at full speed. Both share identical mounting geometry and port positions.

🔩

316 Stainless Steel

Full 316 SS housing and structural components provide corrosion resistance across tap water, seawater, and light chemical environments without coatings or surface treatment.

📏

Two Displacement Options

4.6 cc/rev for lower flow and reduced motor size; 6.0 cc/rev for maximum throughput at 12 L/min. Both displacements use identical mounting geometry, simplifying system design and spares management.

🔄

Boosted and Unboosted

2-port unboosted (PC/PA) for tank-fed HPU installations and 3-port boosted (PB) for higher-speed applications or installations with elevated supply pressure above 2.5 bar.

💧

Zero Oil Contamination

No hydraulic oil anywhere in the circuit. Zero Fisheries Act spill liability, zero oil disposal cost, and zero risk of process fluid contamination in food, water, or nuclear environments.

⚡

Low Pulsation Design

Multiple axial piston geometry produces inherently smooth, low-pulsation flow. Proportional flow control is achieved simply by adjusting shaft speed via VFD drive on the motor.

🏭

ATEX II 2GD Certified

Approved to Directive 94/9/EC for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust explosive atmospheres. T6 temperature class, ambient operating range +1°C to +40°C.

☢️

F4N Fit For Nuclear

F4N accreditation for nuclear environments where oil-free hydraulic power eliminates fire risk and radiological contamination pathways in safety-critical circuits.

🌊

Seawater Compatible

Standard construction handles seawater. Noble material internal packages are available for extended saltwater service life. Specify fluid code S when ordering.

Displacement Selection

4.6 cc/rev or 6.0 cc/rev:
Which P6 Do You Need?

The P6 is unique in the compact end of the WaterQuest range by offering two discrete displacement variants within the same pump body. The choice affects motor sizing, flow output, and power consumption across the operating range.

6.0 cc/rev

Maximum Flow Variant

PC160-6.0W / PA160-6.0W / PB160-6.0W

Choose 6.0 cc/rev when maximum flow throughput is the primary requirement. At 1750 rpm unboosted this variant delivers 10.5 L/min at low pressure, declining to 8.5 L/min at 160 bar. At 2000 rpm boosted, peak flow reaches 12 L/min. The 6.0cc variant requires a larger motor frame and higher drive power across all pressure points.

Maximum water flow at 2000 rpm boosted: 12 L/min at low pressure.
At 1750 rpm unboosted: approximately 10.5 L/min at 20 bar, 8.5 L/min at 160 bar.
Maximum input torque at 160 bar, 1500 rpm: 16.1 Nm.
Maximum input power at 1750 rpm, 160 bar: approximately 3.0 kW.
Pick-a-Pack HPU motor assemblies range from P6-060-M04 (0.37 kW at 10 bar) through P6-060-M10 (3.0 kW at 160 bar).
Port Data (PC160-6.0W / PA160-6.0W)Pressure Port: 3/8" BSPP  |  T Port: 1/2" BSPP (unboosted)  |  T Port: 3/8" BSPP (boosted case drain)
4.6 cc/rev

Reduced Flow Variant

PC160-4.6W / PA160-4.6W / PB160-4.6W

Choose 4.6 cc/rev when lower flow is adequate but you still want the larger P6 body for its port sizing and mounting pattern. At 1750 rpm unboosted the 4.6cc variant delivers approximately 8.0 L/min at low pressure. Input power and torque are proportionally lower, allowing a smaller motor at the same pressure rating.

Maximum water flow at 1750 rpm: approximately 8.0 L/min at 20 bar.
At 1500 rpm: approximately 6.9 L/min at 20 bar, declining to 5.0 L/min at 160 bar.
Maximum input torque at 160 bar, 1500 rpm: 12.8 Nm (21% lower than the 6.0cc variant).
Maximum input power at 1750 rpm, 160 bar: approximately 2.4 kW vs 3.0 kW for 6.0cc, enabling a smaller motor frame.
Pick-a-Pack HPU motor assemblies: P6-046-M04 through P6-046-M09. Both PC/PA and PB configurations available.

Inlet Configuration

Unboosted or Boosted:
P6 Inlet Requirements

Like the P3, the P6 is available in both 2-port unboosted (PC/PA) and 3-port boosted (PB) configurations. The decision follows the same engineering logic: inlet supply conditions and required shaft speed ceiling determine the correct choice.

2-Port Unboosted

PC160 / PA160

Standard configuration for tank-fed HPU installations and low-pressure mains supply. Maximum 1800 rpm.

Supply from local tank or low-pressure mains. Maximum inlet pressure 2.5 bar at the pump inlet at all times.
Two ports only: P (pressure outlet) and T (tank inlet). Pump cannot lift water; inlet must remain positive under all dynamic conditions.
Supply pipework must be maximum bore and minimum length. Inlet flow must be at least 20% above maximum delivery flow at the lowest tank level.
Do not place a filter between the tank and pump unless a negative pressure protection switch is installed. Filter all water entering the tank.
PC = clockwise shaft rotation, PA = anti-clockwise. Reversing port connections or shaft rotation direction causes immediate pump failure.
Maximum shaft speed: 1800 rpm unboosted.

3-Port Boosted

PB160

Required when inlet supply pressure is 2.5 bar or above, or when shaft speeds above 1800 rpm are needed for maximum flow output. Bi-directional.

Three ports: pressure outlet (P), water inlet, and a separate case drain port (T). Internal leakage is routed to a low-pressure sump or drain through the T port.
Minimum inlet supply pressure: 2.5 bar at all times, from a high-pressure main, a water column exceeding 25 metres, or an external boost pump.
Minimum positive inlet pressure must remain at least 2.0 bar above T-port (case drain) pressure. Maximum casing pressure at T port: 2.5 bar.
Separating leakage from the inlet prevents low-pressure cavitation, significantly extends shaft seal life, and enables operation at higher shaft speeds and duty cycles.
Boosted pumps rotate in either direction. Inlet and outlet pressure ports swap according to shaft rotation direction; design pipework accordingly.
Maximum shaft speed: 2000 rpm boosted vs 1800 rpm unboosted. The 6.0 cc/rev variant at 2000 rpm delivers the full rated 12 L/min.

Engineering Detail

P6 Design and
Construction

Axial Piston Architecture

Multiple pistons distribute load evenly across sliding contact surfaces, producing genuinely smooth, low-pulsation flow at all operating speeds. Water acts as the only coolant and lubricant throughout; no oil, additives, or grease nipples are required at any point in the pump.

Discrete Displacement Selection

Unlike the P3 which offers a single fixed displacement, the P6 allows the designer to select between 4.6 cc/rev and 6.0 cc/rev at the time of order. Both displacement variants share the same body, shaft envelope, port positions, and mounting pattern. This means a system can be designed with a common piping and mounting layout regardless of which displacement is installed.

Port Sizing Step-Up from P3

The P6 introduces larger port sizes compared to the P3. The unboosted pressure port steps up to 3/8" BSPP (vs 1/4" on the P3), and the T port on the unboosted configuration uses 1/2" BSPP. The boosted version uses 3/8" BSPP for both the pressure outlet and the case drain T port. This port step-up reflects the higher flow rates the P6 handles and reduces line velocity in the connecting pipework.

Larger Body Diameter

The P6 has a body outer diameter of 76 mm compared to 63 mm on the P3. This larger body accommodates the greater displacement and allows the improved port sizing described above. The mounting pattern uses 2 holes at 5.5 mm diameter on a 23.9 mm PCD spacing, scaled appropriately for the larger body.

Filtration Requirements

All water entering the P6 must be pre-filtered to 10 micron nominal (25 micron absolute), filter element rating beta-10 = 75 or better. At the higher flow rates the P6 produces, suction pipework sizing is particularly important. Always size the supply pipe to ensure inlet flow is at least 20% above maximum pump delivery flow when the tank is at its lowest operating level.

Temperature and Fluid Compatibility

Full rated performance from 2°C to 50°C. Above 50°C volumetric efficiency reduces; consult WaterQuest. Monopropylene Glycol antifreeze available for sub-zero operation. Glycol 95/5 mixtures are compatible with internal clearance adjustment. The exact operating fluid must be specified on all enquiries and orders; changes to fluid chemistry can affect seal material selection and noble material requirements for seawater service.

Frequently Asked Questions

P6 Pump
Technical FAQ

Choose 6.0 cc/rev when you need maximum flow of up to 12 L/min at full pressure, and your motor and drive system can accommodate up to 3.8 kW at peak demand. Choose 4.6 cc/rev when your required flow is lower and you want to use a smaller motor frame, or when you are designing to a tighter power budget. Both displacements use identical body dimensions, port positions, and mounting patterns, so the piping and mechanical installation is identical regardless of displacement selected. Both are available in PC, PA, and PB configurations.

The P6 uses significantly larger ports than the P3 to accommodate higher flow rates without excessive line velocity. The P3 unboosted configuration uses a 1/4" BSPP pressure port and 3/8" BSPP tank port. The P6 unboosted configuration steps up to a 3/8" BSPP pressure port and 1/2" BSPP tank port. The P6 boosted configuration uses 3/8" BSPP for both the pressure outlet and the case drain T port. Always size connecting pipework to match these port sizes as a minimum; larger bore pipe is better on the supply side.

The P6 at 6.0 cc/rev reaches a peak overall efficiency of approximately 85.5% at 60 to 80 bar and 1500 rpm. This is marginally below the P3 peak of 86%, but the P6 maintains high efficiency across a broader pressure band, staying above 80% from 40 bar through to 120 bar at 1500 rpm. The 1000 rpm curve shows a steeper drop-off above 100 bar, which is typical of larger displacement pumps at lower shaft speeds where relative leakage losses are higher.

Yes. The P6 Pick-a-Pack system is available with either the S25 (25-litre) or S55 (55-litre) 316 stainless steel reservoir. The S55 at 350 x 550 x 1050 mm provides larger working volume for higher duty cycle applications or systems where significant return flow volumes need to be accommodated. For intermittent duty applications the S25 is often sufficient. Consult WaterQuest for reservoir sizing advice based on your specific duty cycle and flow requirements.

The DN3 valve range is rated to 10 L/min and 160 bar. C01, C02, and C03 circuits use this range and will suit the 4.6cc P6 at many operating points. For the 6.0cc P6 at higher speeds where flow exceeds 10 L/min, the C04 through C07 manifold circuits using the DN6 valve range are required; these are rated to 22 L/min at 160 bar, which comfortably covers the full P6 output range. Specify the DN6 manifold options (C04 to C07) if you are designing for operation above 10 L/min.
