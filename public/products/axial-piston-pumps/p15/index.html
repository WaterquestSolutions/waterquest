Why the P15

Three Displacements.
One Industrial Body.

The P15 represents a significant step up from the compact P6 range. At 6 kg it is the lightest pump in the range capable of industrial-scale flow, and the three-displacement offering gives system designers genuine flexibility to match flow, motor size, and drive power to specific application requirements without changing the pump body or piping.

🔩

316 Stainless Steel

Full 316 SS construction throughout. Corrosion-resistant across tap water, seawater, technical water, and light chemical environments without coatings, platings, or surface treatment.

🔢

Three Displacement Options

12, 15, and 19 cc/rev share the same body, shaft geometry, port positions, and mounting pattern. The designer selects displacement to match system flow and motor frame requirements.

🔄

Boosted and Unboosted

Both 2-port unboosted (PC/PA) and 3-port boosted (PB) configurations are available across all three displacements, covering the full range of installation conditions and shaft speed requirements.

💧

Zero Oil Contamination

No hydraulic oil at any point. Zero Fisheries Act spill liability, zero oil disposal cost, and zero process contamination risk in water treatment, food, and nuclear applications.

⚡

Low Pulsation Flow

Multiple axial pistons produce inherently smooth output. VFD speed control on the motor provides proportional flow control without any additional valve complexity.

🏭

ATEX II 2GD Certified

Approved to Directive 94/9/EC for Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust explosive atmospheres. T6 temperature class, ambient range +1°C to +40°C.

☢️

F4N Fit For Nuclear

F4N accreditation confirms suitability for nuclear environments where oil-free operation eliminates both fire risk and radiological contamination pathways.

🌊

Seawater Compatible

Standard construction handles seawater. Noble material internal packages available for long-term saltwater service. Specify fluid code S when ordering.

Unboosted or Boosted:
P15 Inlet Requirements

The P15 follows the same inlet configuration logic as the P3 and P6. Unboosted (PC/PA) suits most HPU power pack installations. Boosted (PB) unlocks higher shaft speeds and extends shaft seal life for high duty cycle applications.

2-Port Unboosted

PC160 / PA160

Standard configuration for tank-fed HPU installations and low-pressure mains supply. PC = clockwise, PA = anti-clockwise. Maximum 1800 rpm.

Supply from local tank or low-pressure mains. Maximum inlet pressure 2.5 bar at the pump inlet at all times.
Two ports: P (pressure outlet) and T (tank inlet). Pump cannot lift water. Inlet pressure must remain positive under all dynamic operating conditions.

3-Port Boosted

PB160

Required when inlet supply pressure is 2.5 bar or above, or when shaft speeds above 1800 rpm are needed. Bi-directional. Significant body size step-up from the compact range.

Three ports: pressure outlet (P), water inlet, and a separate case drain (T). Internal leakage is routed through the T port to a low-pressure sump or drain.
Minimum inlet supply pressure: 2.5 bar at all times, from a high-pressure main, a water column exceeding 25 metres, or an external boost pump feeding the inlet.
Minimum positive inlet pressure must remain at least 2.0 bar above T-port (case drain) pressure. Maximum casing pressure at T port: 2.5 bar.
Separating leakage from the inlet eliminates cavitation risk inside the casing, significantly extends shaft seal life, and enables high duty cycle operation at higher shaft speeds.
Boosted pumps rotate in either direction. Inlet and outlet pressure ports change with shaft rotation direction; design and label pipework accordingly.

Engineering Detail

P15 Design and
Construction

Significant Body Step-Up

The P15 is a substantially larger pump than the compact P6. Body outer diameter grows to 114 mm from 76 mm on the P6, the shaft increases to 18.99/18.97 mm with a 6x6 mm key (vs 8.99/8.97 mm and 3x3 key on the P3 and P6), and the port face uses a 4-hole bolt pattern. At 6 kg it is nearly three times the weight of the P6 at 2.2 kg.

Three Displacements, One Body

12, 15, and 19 cc/rev share identical external dimensions, shaft envelope, and port positions. A system can be designed with a common piping layout, bell housing, and mounting arrangement regardless of which displacement is specified. This simplifies spares management and allows a displacement change without modifying the rest of the system.

Port Size Consistency

Unlike the compact range where port sizes vary between unboosted and boosted configurations, the P15 unboosted uses G3/4" BSPP on both P and T ports. The boosted PB uses G3/4" BSPP on the pressure outlet and G1/2" BSPP on the case drain T port. The larger port sizes on the P15 are correctly sized for flow rates up to 37.2 L/min without excessive line velocity.

Motor and Coupling Considerations

The P15 connects to a B5-flange electric motor via bell housings and 3-part gear couplings. At the motor power levels required for the 19cc variant at full pressure (up to 11 kW), motor frame sizing and coupling alignment become more critical. The pump cannot accept induced axial or radial shaft loads at any displacement. Always use a 3-part gear coupling unless concentricity is within 0.05 mm, in which case a direct inline assembly is permissible.

Filtration at Higher Flow Rates

All water entering the P15 must be pre-filtered to 10 micron nominal (25 micron absolute), beta-10 = 75 minimum. At flow rates up to 37.2 L/min the supply pipework bore becomes especially important. Size all supply piping to ensure inlet flow rate is at least 20% above maximum pump delivery at the lowest tank level. A flow rate that would appear marginal on a compact pump can become a serious cavitation risk on a larger pump running at speed.

Temperature and Fluid Compatibility

Full rated performance from 2°C to 50°C. Above 50°C volumetric efficiency reduces; consult WaterQuest for the maximum temperature application. Monopropylene Glycol antifreeze is available for sub-zero installations. The pump operates on glycol 95/5 mixtures with internal clearance adjustment. Seawater service requires noble material internal packages. The exact operating fluid must be specified on all enquiries and orders.

The P15 pairs with either the S55 (55-litre) or S90 (90-litre) 316 stainless steel reservoir. The S90 is the largest reservoir in the standard Pick-a-Pack range and is appropriate for high duty cycle applications or systems with large return volumes at P15 flow rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

P15 Pump
Technical FAQ

Start from your required flow rate at your target operating pressure. The 19cc variant maximises flow and is the correct choice when you need up to 37.2 L/min at full speed. If your flow requirement is lower, stepping down to 15cc or 12cc allows use of a smaller motor frame, reducing both installed cost and energy consumption. All three displacements share the same body, mounting, and port positions, so you can change displacement at design time without modifying pipework or installation. Contact WaterQuest for a specific motor pump assembly recommendation based on your pressure and flow operating point.

The P15 is substantially larger than the P6. The body outer diameter grows from 76 mm on the P6 to 114 mm on the P15. The shaft increases from 8.99/8.97 mm with a 3x3 mm key to 18.99/18.97 mm with a 6x6 mm key. Weight increases from 2.2 kg (P6) to 6 kg (P15). Port sizes step up from 3/8" and 1/2" BSPP on the P6 unboosted to G3/4" BSPP on both ports of the P15 unboosted configuration. This scaling is appropriate for the significant jump in flow rate, from 12 L/min maximum on the P6 to 37.2 L/min maximum on the P15.

At 160 bar with the 19cc displacement, the P15 requires an 11 kW motor in a 160M frame (assembly code P15-019-M14). At 15cc and 160 bar the motor requirement drops to 7.5 kW in a 132M frame. At 12cc and 160 bar approximately 5.5 kW in a 132S frame is needed. If you are operating below 160 bar, smaller motor frames are available across all three displacements. The Pick-a-Pack programme covers frame sizes from 80 Frame (0.55 kW) through 160M Frame (11 kW). Consult WaterQuest for the full motor selection table covering all pressure, displacement, and speed combinations.

The P15 at 19cc and 1500 rpm reaches a peak overall efficiency of approximately 85% at 80 bar. At 1000 rpm the peak efficiency of around 84% occurs at 60 bar, with a steeper decline above 80 bar than at 1500 rpm. The 500 rpm curve peaks at about 72.5% efficiency at 60 bar and drops significantly above 80 bar, confirming that the P15 at 19cc displacement should not be used at sustained high pressure and 500 rpm in applications where efficiency is important. For sustained high-pressure duty the 1000 to 1500 rpm operating range is preferred.

The standard P15 construction will operate on seawater, but for long-term offshore service where corrosion exposure is continuous and maintenance access is limited, a noble material internal package is recommended. Specify fluid code S when ordering and WaterQuest will configure the appropriate internal material selection. The 316 SS body provides excellent resistance in saline environments. All enquiries for seawater applications should include the expected chloride concentration, operating temperature, and duty cycle so that the correct material specification can be confirmed.
