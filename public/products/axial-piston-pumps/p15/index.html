Why the P15

Three Displacements. One Industrial Body.

The P15’s displacement choices let you match delivered flow to the machine’s demand while using a common pump format. Use the curves and connection drawings to select the drive and installation around the operating point.

316 Stainless Steel

Specify water chemistry and temperature for material and seal selection. Seawater requires an application-specific configuration.

Three Displacement Options

12, 15 and 19 cm³/rev (0.732, 0.915 and 1.159 in³/rev, respectively) share the same body, shaft geometry, port positions, and mounting pattern. The designer selects displacement to match system flow and motor frame requirements.

Boosted and Unboosted

Both 2-port unboosted (PC/PA) and 3-port boosted (PB) configurations are available across all three displacements, covering the full range of installation conditions and shaft speed requirements.

Hydraulic oil removed from the circuit

Remove oil and its handling tasks from this circuit. Specify water quality and the service routine.

Low Pulsation Flow

Multiple axial pistons produce inherently smooth output. VFD speed control on the motor provides proportional flow control without any additional valve complexity.

Hazardous-area documentation

For hazardous-area duty, request current conformity documents for the exact model, configuration and intended zone. Include the installation requirements with your enquiry.

Inlet Configuration

Unboosted or Boosted: P15 Inlet Requirements

The P15 follows the same inlet configuration logic as the P3 and P6.

2-Port Unboosted

PC160 / PA160

Standard configuration for tank-fed HPU installations and low-pressure mains supply. PC = clockwise, PA = anti-clockwise. Maximum 1800 rpm.

Supply from local tank or low-pressure mains. Maximum inlet pressure 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) at the pump inlet at all times.
Two ports: P (pressure outlet) and T (tank inlet). Pump cannot lift water. Inlet pressure must remain positive under all dynamic operating conditions.
Supply pipework must be maximum bore and minimum length. Inlet flow must be at least 20% above maximum pump delivery flow when the tank is at its lowest operating level.
Do not place a filter between the tank and pump unless a negative pressure protection switch is installed. Filter all water entering the tank at that point.
PC = clockwise shaft rotation, PA = anti-clockwise. Reversing port connections or running shaft in the wrong direction causes immediate pump failure.
Maximum shaft speed: 1800 rpm unboosted.

3-Port Boosted

PB160

Required when inlet supply pressure is 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) or above, or when shaft speeds above 1800 rpm are needed. Bi-directional.

Three ports: pressure outlet (P), water inlet, and a separate case drain (T). Internal leakage is routed through the T port to a low-pressure sump or drain.
Minimum inlet supply pressure: 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) at all times, from a high-pressure main, a water column exceeding 25 metres (82 ft), or an external boost pump feeding the inlet.

Engineering Detail

P15 Design and Construction

Significant Body Step-Up

The P15 is a substantially larger pump than the compact P6. Body outer diameter grows to 114 mm (4.49 in) from 76 mm (2.99 in) on the P6, the shaft increases to 18.99/18.97 mm (0.748/0.747 in) with a 6x6 mm (0.236x0.236 in) key (vs 8.99/8.97 mm (0.354/0.353 in) and 3x3 key on the P3 and P6), and the port face uses a 4-hole bolt pattern. At 6 kg (13.2 lb) it is nearly three times the weight of the P6 at 2.2 kg (4.85 lb).

Three Displacements, One Body

12, 15 and 19 cm³/rev (0.732, 0.915 and 1.159 in³/rev, respectively) share identical external dimensions, shaft envelope, and port positions.

Port Size Consistency

Unlike the compact range where port sizes vary between unboosted and boosted configurations, the P15 unboosted uses G3/4" BSPP on both P and T ports. The boosted PB uses G3/4" BSPP on the pressure outlet and G1/2" BSPP on the case drain T port. The larger port sizes on the P15 are correctly sized for flow rates up to 37.2 L/min (9.83 US gpm) without excessive line velocity.

Motor and Coupling Considerations

The P15 connects to a B5-flange electric motor via bell housings and 3-part gear couplings. At the motor power levels required for the 19cc (1.16 in³) variant at full pressure (up to 11 kW (14.8 hp)), motor frame sizing and coupling alignment become more critical. The pump cannot accept induced axial or radial shaft loads at any displacement. Always use a 3-part gear coupling unless concentricity is within 0.05 mm (0.00197 in), in which case a direct inline assembly is permissible.

Filtration at Higher Flow Rates

All water entering the P15 must be pre-filtered to 10 micron nominal (25 micron absolute), beta-10 = 75 minimum. At flow rates up to 37.2 L/min (9.83 US gpm) the supply pipework bore becomes especially important. Size all supply piping to ensure inlet flow rate is at least 20% above maximum pump delivery at the lowest tank level. A flow rate that would appear marginal on a compact pump can become a serious cavitation risk on a larger pump running at speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

P15 Pump Technical FAQ

Start from your required flow rate at your target operating pressure. The 19cc (1.16 in³) variant maximizes flow and is the correct choice when you need up to 37.2 L/min (9.83 US gpm) at full speed. If your flow requirement is lower, stepping down to 15cc (0.915 in³) or 12cc (0.732 in³) allows use of a smaller motor frame, reducing both installed cost and energy consumption.

The P15 is substantially larger than the P6. The body outer diameter grows from 76 mm on the P6 to 114 mm on the P15. The shaft increases from 8.99/8.97 mm with a 3x3 mm key to 18.99/18.97 mm with a 6x6 mm key. Weight increases from 2.2 kg (4.85 lb) (P6) to 6 kg (13.2 lb) (P15). Port sizes step up from 3/8" and 1/2" BSPP on the P6 unboosted to G3/4" BSPP on both ports of the P15 unboosted configuration. This scaling is appropriate for the significant jump in flow rate, from 12 L/min (3.17 US gpm) maximum on the P6 to 37.2 L/min (9.83 US gpm) maximum on the P15.
