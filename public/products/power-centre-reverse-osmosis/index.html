Follow the feed and brine paths

The pump supplies RO feed pressure while the energy-recovery function uses pressure available in the concentrate stream. Confirm both flow paths, recovery ratio and pressure conditions. Auxiliary pumping may still be required for the complete arrangement.

Set a consistent energy boundary

Compare input at the same product-water rate, feed conditions and membrane duty. State whether pretreatment, booster pumps and other auxiliaries are included. Reference production and recovery curves are useful selection inputs, not guaranteed plant output.

Specify saline service deliberately

Review feed and brine chemistry, cleaning agents and flushing procedure against the selected materials and seals. Include pressure protection, instrumentation and service access. Confirm the operating envelope for the exact Power Centre configuration.

Technology

Four Components. One Unit. The Energy Recovery Loop.

Component 01
💪

High-Pressure Pump

Axial piston, product-lubricated. Water-based lubrication throughout. No oil changes.

Component 02
♻️

Energy Recovery Device

Converts high-pressure brine exhaust energy directly back to shaft power. No separate unit.

Component 03
⚡

Booster Pump

Driven directly by recovered brine energy via shaft coupling. No separate motor needed.

Component 04
🔌

Electric Motor

50 Hz and 60 Hz compatible. Direct drive. No pulleys, belts, or speed reducers required.

The Energy Recovery Loop

Pre-filtered feed water enters. Fresh permeate exits. Recovered brine energy feeds back into the system shaft.
