Power Centre · Integrated RO energy recovery

Recover the pressure.
Reduce the demand.

Your concentrate stream still has energy. Put it back to work.

The Power Centre combines a high-pressure feed pump with a brine energy-recovery motor. Recovered torque helps drive the pump, reducing the electrical input needed for the selected reverse-osmosis duty.

Less demand on the electric drive

High-pressure concentrate turns the recovery motor and returns power to the common shaft. This can lower running costs and allow a smaller electric drive for the same defined RO duty.

Fewer separate recovery components

The integrated arrangement avoids a separate energy-recovery drive motor and a separate high-pressure recovery booster. It can reduce the supports, interconnections and space needed for the recovery function.

Compare equal net water output, feed conditions and membrane duty. Include upstream feed pumping, pretreatment and other auxiliary loads. Manufacturer energy comparison basis.

Follow the energy

The brine helps power the feed.

01 / PRESSURISE

Feed the membranes

The axial-piston pump raises the conditioned feed to the pressure required by the RO membrane train.

02 / RECOVER

Use the concentrate pressure

High-pressure brine leaving the membranes passes through the integrated hydraulic motor and contributes torque to the drive shaft.

03 / ASSIST

Supply the remaining power

The electric motor makes up the balance. Low-pressure brine leaves through the specified backpressure and discharge arrangement.

The upstream feed/boost pump has a different job: maintaining inlet pressure. It remains part of the complete plant even though the integrated recovery arrangement needs no separate high-pressure recovery booster.

Compact by design

Make room for a simpler installation.

Bring the drive motor, flexible coupling, pump and recovery motor together on a supported frame. The compact arrangement suits packaged RO systems and sites where space matters, including ships and offshore facilities.

  • Four main process paths: low-pressure feed in, high-pressure feed out, high-pressure brine in and low-pressure brine out.
  • Complete the connections: include the case drains, pressure-switch ports and auxiliary connections shown on the selected drawing.
  • Protect the inlet: the published PC300 arrangement requires at least 3.5 bar (50.8 psi) at the inlet pressure-switch port, with shutdown on low inlet pressure.

Use model-specific mounting, speed, pressure, filtration and port requirements. Provide the required rear support, drainage, instrumentation and pressure protection.

Match the water. Plan the service.

Water lubrication.
Practical access.

Process water lubricates the pump and recovery-motor working surfaces, removing hydraulic lubricating oil and the related oil-change task from those wetted units.

Duplex stainless options, seals and connections are selected around the feedwater, concentrate and cleaning conditions. Plan access for inspection, flushing and service from the outset.

Supplied range literature describes service intervals up to 8,000 hours when its specifications are met. Confirm the applicable interval and service-exchange availability for the quoted unit. Motor bearings and other plant equipment retain their own maintenance requirements.
