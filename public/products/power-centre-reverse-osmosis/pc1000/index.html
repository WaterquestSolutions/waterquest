PC1000: feed pumping and brine energy recovery

What it does: Combines high-pressure RO feed pumping with brine energy recovery.

Where it’s used: Seawater reverse-osmosis plants with defined feed, permeate and recovery duties.

Why water: Recover useful brine pressure through a water-lubricated drive arrangement.

Engineering checks

Confirm before selection

  • Pressure rating. The specification table lists 85 bar (1,233 psi) maximum; the circuit description states 80 bar (1,160 psi). Confirm the approved maximum for the selected unit.
  • Case-drain flange rating. The connection table states 300 psi (20.7 bar) class, while the drawing notation requires clarification. Confirm the correct flange standard before ordering.

Plan the installation

Connections, support and protection.

Allow space for service, support the rear of the Power Centre and keep case drainage separate from the pressurized feed.

01

Protect the inlet

Maintain at least 3.5 bar (50.8 psi) at the inlet pressure switch port. Interlock the drive to stop if inlet pressure falls below the specified minimum.

02

Mount and support

Install horizontally with a flexible gear coupling. The rear of the unit requires support. Verify alignment and final motor/frame dimensions.

03

Route drains correctly

Pipe internal leakage to a low-pressure sump or drain. Confirm allowable case pressure, discharge backpressure and filtration requirements.

Do not scale from the screen. Confirm the supplied assembly, dimensions and motor selection.

Recover pressure. Reduce demand.

Put brine energy back to work.

The Power Centre recovers RO reject-stream pressure through an axial piston motor, reducing the electric motor’s load.

01

One integrated assembly

Two high-pressure pumps and the energy recovery motor share a common mechanical drive. The recovery function needs no separate electric drive or separate pressure-boost pump.

02

Designed around seawater

Duplex shafts, pistons and housings provide the stated material basis. Water lubricates the bearing surfaces, eliminating lubricating-oil changes within the pump and recovery unit.

03

Matched to your RO duty

Specify flow, salinity, temperature and recovery together. Shaft speed controls output flow, while the recovery ratio is configured at the order stage.
