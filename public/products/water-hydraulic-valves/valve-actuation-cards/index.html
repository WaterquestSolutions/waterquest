Engineering Design

Key Design Features

⚡

PWM Pull-Hold Sequence

The card provides an initial 24V pull-in supply for 100ms, then reduces average voltage to 15V for a further 2 seconds, then drops to 10V for the holding condition. This sequence minimises coil temperature and reduces ongoing power consumption significantly.

🌡️

Coil Temperature Protection

The PCB controls the duration of supply to ensure the maximum coil temperature of the 12V DC coil is not exceeded. The solenoid should not be switched on/off continuously with a duty cycle shorter than 1 second.

🔌

PLC Signal Compatible

The signal supply can be derived from the last 2 terminals of the board or supplied directly from a PLC output. Requires 6mA at 24V or 1mA at 5V from the PLC output. LED indicators show energised and holding states.

🛡️

Interference Caution

Once energised, the card remains in holding condition with only a 1.3V signal voltage. Signal cables must be routed carefully to avoid interference that could induce voltages high enough to trigger the signal.

🏗️

DIN Rail Mounting

Optional DIN rail mounting available for 1, 2, or 3 cards. Mounting-only brackets (004-101/102/103) also available for use with existing cards. 48V coil version requires both 24V and 48V 2.5A power supplies.

✅

Confirm supplier programme status and scope

Request current manufacturer quality-system documentation and confirm the project-specific approval requirements. Specially programmed versions available for 28V automotive applications.
