Pressure control

Pressure Reducing Valves

Give each function the pressure it needs.

Pressure reducing valves limit downstream pressure in multi-function water hydraulic circuits. They can maintain the controlled pressure at zero flow, supporting cylinder-force control at a stall or end stop.

Independent pressure levels

Control at zero demand

03

Relieving function

Near the setting, the valve restricts inlet flow and relieves the service line to tank.
