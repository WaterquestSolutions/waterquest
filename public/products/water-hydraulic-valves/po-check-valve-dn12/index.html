DN12 Pilot-Operated Check Valve

Secure holding at higher flow.

A pilot-operated check valve for load and pressure holding up to 60 L/min. The standard 2.3:1 pilot ratio helps release a held load using a lower actuation pressure, with stand-alone and manifold mounting options.
