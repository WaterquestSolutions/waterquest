Engineering Design

Key Design Features

🔲

Manifold Block Design

Multiple 2/2 valve functions can be arranged from a single stainless steel manifold block, reducing the cost and complexity of fittings and pipework. Available in 1 to 5 station configurations for both DN9 and DN25 sizes.

💧

Wet Pin Solenoid

The pilot section wet pin solenoid actuator has no dynamic sealing, reducing wear and potential leak points while producing adequate direct actuation force up to 160 bar (2,321 psi) with a 2.5mm (0.0984 in) pilot orifice.

📐

DN9 and DN25 Sizes

Two orifice sizes available. DN9 with 9mm gallery and 3/8 inch (9.525 mm) BSPP porting handles up to 60 L/min (15.9 US gpm). DN25 with 20mm gallery and 1 inch (25.4 mm) BSPP porting handles up to 120 L/min (31.7 US gpm) for high-flow circuit applications.

⬆️⬇️

N/O and N/C Configurations

Both normally open and normally closed configurations available. Porting orientation changes with spring condition. All configurations available in stand-alone or manifold-mount variants.

🏗️

1 to 5 Station Manifolds

Stainless steel manifold blocks available in 1 to 5 stations for both DN9 and DN25 valve sizes. Manifolds accept all 2/2 N/O and N/C configurations in solenoid or pilot actuation. Ordered separately under manifold ordering codes 500-009 to 500-027.
