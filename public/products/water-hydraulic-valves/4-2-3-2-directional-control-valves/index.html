4/2 & 3/2 Directional Control Valves

Route water where it needs to go.

Directional valves for low-viscosity water circuits, with 4/2 and 3/2 functions and a choice of stand-alone, manifold or CETOP 3 mounting. Solenoid operation includes a manual override.
