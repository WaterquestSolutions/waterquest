Engineering Design

Key Design Features

🔒

Bubble Tight 100% Seal

The PO Check provides bubble-tight 100% seal operation for load holding and pressure holding applications. Unlike standard directional valves, this valve provides complete leak-free isolation when de-piloted.

⚖️

2.3:1 Pilot Ratio

The standard 2.3:1 ratio allows the piston pressure to be overcome by a much lower pilot actuation pressure, enabling actuation with minimum input pressure and overcoming amplification pressures caused by cylinder differential areas. Ratio can be modified on request.

💧

Multi-Fluid Pilot

Can be actuated by water, oil, or compressed air pilot. The valve is double sealed to ensure cross contamination does not occur between the working fluid and pilot fluid. Ideal for systems with mixed fluid types.

🏗️

Manifold Mount Available

Available as stand-alone or manifold-mounted. The manifold block can be configured to offer double port checked functions, providing load holding on both extend and retract sides of a cylinder.

📐

Compact 316 SS Body

All stainless steel construction. Ø50mm body diameter, 118.3mm overall length. Inlet and outlet both 1/2 inch (12.7 mm) BSPP, pilot port 1/4 inch (6.35 mm) BSPP. M5x6 deep mounting holes.

✅

Confirm current quality-system documentation

Request current manufacturer quality-system documentation and confirm the project-specific approval requirements. The standard 2.3:1 pilot ratio can be modified for specific system requirements on request.
