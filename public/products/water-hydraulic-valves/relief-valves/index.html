Selection considerations

  • Confirm DN12 capacity and the allowable inlet pressure for the selected configuration.
  • A drip rate can occur below the set pressure. Specify a separate isolation function where zero leakage is required.
  • Confirm certification and suitability separately for any safety-related duty.
