Engineering Design

Key Design Features

🌊

High Flow Capability

At 0 to 140 L/min (0 to 37 US gpm) through an 18mm feed gallery and 3/4 inch (19.05 mm) BSP ports, the DN16 handles large-circuit flow rates that smaller direct-acting solenoid valves cannot. Suitable for large actuators, multiple cylinder circuits, and high-demand industrial applications.

💧

Multi-Media Pilot Actuation

Actuation by water, oil, or compressed air pilot. The water pilot version is available with an integral DN3 solenoid-operated pilot valve. For oil pilot, the actuator has dual seal and air vent design to prevent cross contamination.

🎛️

Adjustable Pilot Pressure

The pilot piston can be sized to vary the pilot actuation pressure between 2 and 80 bar (29 and 1,160 psi). Standard external pilot actuator is designed for 40 bar (580 psi). Custom operating ranges available on request.

⬆️⬇️

Five Functions

Available in: 4/3 with blocked P (AB and T joined at centre), 4/3 open centre (all ports joined), 4/2, 3/2 N/O, and 3/2 N/C. Spring return standard; detent function with pilot return available.

🔧

Integral Pilot Option

The DN16 is available with an integral 3/2 solenoid-operated pilot actuation valve (see 20*EESW for dimensions), providing a self-contained solenoid-piloted directional valve in a single assembly.

✅

Confirm current quality-system documentation

Request current manufacturer quality-system documentation and confirm the project-specific approval requirements. Actuation housing size varies with pilot type and operating pressure.
