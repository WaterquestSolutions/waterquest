Solenoid Actuation Cards

Full pull-in force. Lower holding power.

Electronic actuation cards manage the solenoid energising sequence for compatible directional valves. A timed voltage profile provides initial pull-in force, then reduces the holding voltage to limit coil heating.

Technical specifications

Know the operating envelope.

Standard power supply
Regulated 24 V DC, 5 A
Signal input
5–24 V relative to supply 0 V
PLC input current
6 mA at 24 V / 1 mA at 5 V
Initial energisation
24 V for 100 ms
Second stage
15 V average for 2 seconds
Holding stage
10 V average
Ambient temperature
−40 to 50 °C
Minimum switching cycle
Do not cycle faster than once per second
48 V coil version
Requires 24 V and 48 V, 2.5 A power supplies
Mounting
Board only or optional DIN rail mount

Selection considerations

  • Match the card programme to the coil. The standard sequence uses a compatible 12 V coil with controlled energisation.
  • Once energised, the card may hold with a signal as low as 1.3 V. Route signal wiring to prevent induced voltages.
