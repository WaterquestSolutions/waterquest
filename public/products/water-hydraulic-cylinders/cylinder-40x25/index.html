Areas and conversions use unrounded geometry. The 0.98 value is the manufacturer’s force-calculation factor, not a guaranteed overall energy efficiency. Figures assume zero return-side gauge pressure. For the actual load, assess opposing pressure on its own effective area, friction, buckling, mounting and load guidance.
