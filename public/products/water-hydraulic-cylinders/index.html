Materials

Material Choices for Water Service.

Cylinder Body

316 Stainless Steel

Standard 316 stainless steel construction provides the starting point for water-service material selection. Match the alloy to the water chemistry, temperature and exposure. Chloride-bearing water can still cause localised corrosion; stainless steel is not corrosion-proof.

Wear Surfaces

Engineering Polymer Seals

High-grade engineering polymer seals are part of the standard cylinder construction. Match the exact polymer and seal arrangement to the selected fluid, pressure, temperature and movement rather than treating seal selection as an afterthought.

Seal Package

Construction Matched to the Fluid

Tap-water and seawater duties require different construction decisions. The catalogue offers duplex stainless steel for seawater service and an optional ceramic coating for demanding duties. Review salinity, treatment chemicals and operating temperature before selection.

Actuator Configurations

Single and Double Acting.
Custom Mounting.

Single Acting

Unidirectional Force

Pressure produces the working stroke in one direction. A load, spring or other external force provides return motion, depending on the specified arrangement.

Select the return arrangement and its available force throughout the stroke. Confirm porting, venting and load behaviour for the particular single-acting design.

Double Acting

Bidirectional Control

Pressure can act on either side of the piston to produce extension and retraction. A single rod reduces the effective area on retraction, so push and pull forces differ at the same supply pressure.

Use the complete valve, feedback and load-control arrangement to achieve the required motion. A double-acting cylinder alone does not guarantee precise positioning or safe load holding.

Operational Value

Five Benefits for
Your Operation

💰

Maintenance You Can Plan

Published service intervals provide a starting point for maintenance planning. Compare inspection access, spare parts, operating duty and downtime costs for the proposed installation rather than assuming a fixed saving.

🎯

Force You Can Calculate

Bore, rod and pressure establish the force calculation. Return-side pressure and friction reduce the force available to the load. Check both directions and the complete stroke against the application requirements.

🌿

Remove an Oil-Release Pathway

Using water instead of hydraulic oil addresses one source of oil contamination. Assess the remaining water-release, pressure and process-contamination hazards as part of the installation design.

🔩

Material Selection with Purpose

Select the alloy and seal package for the fluid, temperature and environment. Duplex construction is an option for seawater duties; the standard 316 description is not evidence of suitability for every chloride exposure.

📋

Environmental Decisions with Evidence

Evaluate oil avoidance alongside water treatment, energy use, leakage and maintenance. Use project-specific evidence to support environmental claims rather than treating oil-free operation as automatic compliance.
