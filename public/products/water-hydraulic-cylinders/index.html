Actuator Configurations

Single and Double Acting.
Custom Mounting.

Single Acting

Unidirectional Force

Working stroke in one direction. Return provided by load, spring, or external force. Can be configured as push or pull depending on application requirements.

Ideal for applications where gravity, a spring, or the process load provides the return stroke. Simpler circuit design with a single pressure port and a vent or tank connection.

Double Acting

Bidirectional Control

Two opposing piston surfaces for controlled motion in both directions, converting hydraulic energy into precise mechanical work. Port A extends, Port B retracts.

Full hydraulic control in both directions. Required where precise positioning or controlled retraction forces are needed. Standard for gate and valve actuation applications.
