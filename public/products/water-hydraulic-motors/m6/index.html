Why the M6

Balanced Power
Zero Oil. Higher Torque.

The M6 motor bridges compact and high-torque designs, delivering double the torque of the M3 while maintaining low weight at 1.2 kg for applications requiring more power without sacrificing environmental compliance.

🔧

316 Stainless Steel

Full 316 SS construction throughout. Corrosion-resistant across tap water, seawater, and light chemical environments without coatings or surface treatment.

💧

Zero Oil Contamination

No hydraulic oil at any point. Zero spill liability, zero oil disposal cost, and zero process contamination risk in water treatment, food, and pharmaceutical applications.

⚡

Consistent Power Curve

80% efficiency maintained across 40-100 bar range provides predictable performance for variable-load applications in processing equipment.

🌊

Seawater Compatible

Standard construction handles seawater. Runs on seawater without corrosion concerns, eliminating environmental discharge violations in marine protected areas.

🏭

Higher Torque Output

14.2 Nm torque capacity supports larger conveyor drives, mixers, and material handling systems in food and pharmaceutical facilities.

🔄

Bi-Directional Capability

MB series provides reversible rotation for positioning systems and reversible conveyors without valve manifold complexity.

🛡️

Ceramic Bearing Technology

Sealed bearings eliminate lubrication requirements and extend service beyond 10,000 hours in water hydraulic service.

🔥

Reduced Fire Hazard

Water operation eliminates petroleum fire risk in confined marine spaces and facilities with ignition sources.

Application Engineering

Ideal Use
Cases

The M6 excels in mid-torque applications where oil contamination would compromise safety, product quality, or environmental compliance.

🍕

Food & Beverage Processing

Mixer drives, conveyor systems, and packaging line actuators where oil contact would violate food safety regulations.

⚓

Marine Deck Equipment

Larger winches, hatch actuators, and crane positioning operating in marine protected areas without environmental discharge concerns.

🏭

Wastewater Treatment

Screen drives, screw conveyors, and valve actuators in corrosive process streams requiring higher torque output.

🔬

Laboratory Automation

Large centrifuge drives, automated reactors, and test stand positioning requiring absolute cleanliness.

Pharmaceutical Manufacturing

Granulator drives, coating pan motors, and material transfer systems with FDA-compliant materials.

Material Handling Systems

Belt conveyor drives, bucket elevators, and screw feeders in food, chemical, and pharmaceutical facilities.
