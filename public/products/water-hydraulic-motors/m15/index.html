M Series Water Hydraulic Motor

M15 High-Torque Water Hydraulic Motor

Robust axial piston motor delivering up to 45 Nm continuous torque at 160 bar system pressure. Available in 15cc and 19cc displacement variants for demanding industrial, marine, and municipal applications requiring oil-free rotary power.

Engineering Data

Performance Characteristics

Verified test data for 15cc and 19cc displacement variants across full operating envelope. All curves measured on tap water at 20°C ambient.

Output Power (kW)

Power output increases linearly with pressure. 19cc variant delivers 14 kW at 160 bar and 3000 rpm.

Overall Efficiency (%)

Peak efficiency of 86% achieved between 60-100 bar. Efficiency curve shown for 19cc at 1000 rpm and 1500 rpm.

Output Torque (Nm)

Torque output is proportional to displacement and pressure. 19cc delivers 45 Nm at 160 bar, 15cc delivers 36 Nm.

Input Flow (L/min)

Flow requirements vary with displacement and speed. 19cc at 3000 rpm requires approximately 48 L/min at rated pressure.

Core Capabilities

Engineered for Contamination-Free Power

Purpose-built for environments where hydraulic oil poses environmental, safety, or regulatory risks.

316 Stainless Steel Construction

Corrosion-resistant wetted components suitable for seawater, potable water, and aggressive industrial fluids. Internal contact surfaces use carbon-reinforced PEEK and stainless steel.

High Volumetric Efficiency

Precision-lapped pistons and cylinder block maintain tight clearances for minimal internal leakage. Volumetric efficiency exceeds 94% across operating range.

Robust Bearing System

Ceramic hybrid bearings handle combined radial and axial loads while operating in water environment. Service life exceeds 8,000 hours under rated conditions.

Modular Port Configuration

Standard G3/4" BSPP pressure ports with optional tank port (T Port G1/2" BSPP on select models). Compatible with WQS Stainless fittings and standard industrial hydraulic connections.
