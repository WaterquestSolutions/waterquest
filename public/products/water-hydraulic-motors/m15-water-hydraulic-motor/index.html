Engineering Data

Performance Characteristics

Compare the M15 flow, torque, power and efficiency curves for the stated displacement and speed. Use the conditions identified with the source data when assessing the motor for your operating point.

Output Power (kW)

Power output increases linearly with pressure. 19cc (1.16 in³) variant delivers 14 kW (18.8 hp) at 160 bar (2,321 psi) and 3000 rpm.

Overall Efficiency (%)

Peak efficiency of 86% achieved between 60-100 bar (870-1,450 psi). Efficiency curve shown for 19cc (1.16 in³) at 1000 rpm and 1500 rpm.

Output Torque (Nm)

Torque output is proportional to displacement and pressure. 19cc (1.16 in³) delivers 45 Nm (33.2 lbf·ft) at 160 bar (2,321 psi), 15cc (0.915 in³) delivers 36 Nm (26.6 lbf·ft).

Input Flow (L/min)

Flow requirements vary with displacement and speed. 19cc (1.16 in³) at 3000 rpm requires approximately 48 L/min (12.7 US gpm) at rated pressure.

Core Capabilities

Rotary power with oil out of the working circuit

Purpose-built for environments where hydraulic oil poses environmental, safety, or regulatory risks.

316 Stainless Steel Construction

Select the components in contact with water and their internal sliding materials for the actual fluid and temperature. Share seawater, potable-water or other process requirements with WaterQuest so the appropriate material and seal configuration can be identified.

High Volumetric Efficiency

The piston and cylinder-block interfaces manage leakage through their surface finish and clearances. Use the model’s performance curves at the intended speed and pressure to assess delivered torque and flow demand.

Support the driven load correctly

Review the permitted shaft loads, coupling arrangement and service instructions for the selected motor. A well-supported mechanical connection helps the motor deliver the required rotary duty without imposing unintended loads.

Modular Port Configuration

Standard G3/4 BSPP pressure ports with optional tank port (T Port G1/2 BSPP on select models). Compatible with WQS Stainless fittings and standard industrial hydraulic connections.

Technical Specifications

Dimensions and Mounting

Check the mounting drawing and shaft connection against your driven equipment. MA identifies anticlockwise rotation, MC clockwise rotation and MB bidirectional operation. Use the correct port arrangement for the required direction, and support the driven load so it does not impose radial or axial loads on the motor shaft.
