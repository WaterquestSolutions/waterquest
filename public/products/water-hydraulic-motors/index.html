Water Hydraulic Motors

When the load needs to turn, torque is what matters. Not what's inside the hydraulic circuit.

A winch that stalls, a conveyor that loses speed or a drive that can't overcome its starting load isn't doing its job. You need usable torque, dependable rotation and control throughout the operating cycle. See how water hydraulic motors can deliver those outcomes without petroleum-based hydraulic oil.

Check the starting condition

A motor that meets running power may still be unsuitable for breakaway torque or low-speed control. Use the model’s performance information at the required pressure difference and flow, with load inertia and acceleration included.

Coordinate the mechanical connection

Confirm shaft form, coupling alignment and permitted external loads. Support the driven machine independently where required. Review stopping, overrunning and any mechanical brake or load-holding function as part of the complete drive.

Compare energy at useful output

Assess flow, pressure and measured input over the operating cycle. Include the pump, electrical drive and standby demand if the decision concerns the whole system. A single mechanical-efficiency figure does not describe installed electrical consumption.

Port Configuration

2-Port and 3-Port Motor Configurations

All connections are BSPP parallel threads with high-pressure sealing washers. Do not use taper fittings under any circumstances.

Standard Configuration

2-Port Configuration

Unidirectional rotation with P (pressure) inlet and T (tank return) clearly marked
Max return line pressure 2.5 bar (36.3 psi)
Motor performance adjustable via return flow control
Return line pressure must not exceed 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) at any time
Return line pressure must exceed leakage port pressure at all times
BSPP parallel thread connections with high-pressure sealing washers only
Never connect ports in reverse order. Catastrophic failure will occur.
Bidirectional Configuration

3-Port Configuration

All sizes available in bi-directional configuration with additional T case drain port
Alternating pressure inlet and return ports reverses shaft direction
Both pressure ports can accept full system pressure
Case drain max 2.5 bar (36.3 psi)
Where motor shaft continues to rotate after valve closure, over-run check valves must be incorporated into the circuit design
BSPP parallel thread connections with high-pressure sealing washers only
Do not use taper fittings under any circumstances.

Technical Specifications

Technical Requirements for Water Hydraulic Motors

🔬

Filtration Requirements

All incoming water must be pre-filtered to 10 micron (394 µin) nominal (25 micron (984 µin) absolute), filter element beta10 = 75 or better. Return line filtration is advised on closed loop systems.

🌡️

Operating Temperature Range

Full performance from 2 degrees C to 50 degrees C. Monopropylene Glycol antifreeze available for operation below 0 degrees C. Operation above 50 degrees C is possible; specify maximum temperature on enquiry as volumetric efficiency will be affected.

⚙️

Shaft Loading

Radial and axial loads are not permitted on the motor output shaft. Use a flexible gear coupling wherever possible to protect shaft bearings and extend service life.

🔄

Start Torque Requirements

M3 and M6 motors require a minimum 25 bar (363 psi) to start unloaded. Larger motors start above 12 bar (174 psi). For full-load starting, use 60% of dynamic torque shown in performance graphs.

⚠️

Over-Run Protection

Where the motor shaft continues to rotate after valve closure, over-run check valves must be incorporated into the circuit design to prevent cavitation damage.

💧

Fluid Compatibility

Clean tap water, deionised water, demineralised water, seawater, and glycol-water mixtures. No oil, no petroleum additives, no glycol-only fluids. Contact WaterQuest for unusual fluid compositions.

ATEX Certification for Hazardous Areas

For hazardous-area service, request current conformity documentation for the exact motor configuration, equipment category and intended zone. Include the installation’s approval requirements in your enquiry.
