Technical Specifications

Technical Requirements for
Water Hydraulic Motors

🔬

Filtration Requirements

All incoming water must be pre-filtered to 10 micron nominal (25 micron absolute), filter element beta10 = 75 or better. Return line filtration is advised on closed loop systems.

🌡️

Operating Temperature Range

Full performance from 2 degrees C to 50 degrees C. Monopropylene Glycol antifreeze available for operation below 0 degrees C. Operation above 50 degrees C is possible; specify maximum temperature on enquiry as volumetric efficiency will be affected.

⚙️

Shaft Loading

Radial and axial loads are not permitted on the motor output shaft. Use a flexible gear coupling wherever possible to protect shaft bearings and extend service life.

🔄

Start Torque Requirements

M3 and M6 motors require a minimum 25 bar to start unloaded. Larger motors start above 12 bar. For full-load starting, use 60% of dynamic torque shown in performance graphs.

⚠️

Over-Run Protection

Where the motor shaft continues to rotate after valve closure, over-run check valves must be incorporated into the circuit design to prevent cavitation damage.

💧

Fluid Compatibility

Clean tap water, deionised water, demineralised water, seawater, and glycol-water mixtures. No oil, no petroleum additives, no glycol-only fluids. Contact WaterQuest for unusual fluid compositions.

ATEX Certification for Hazardous Areas

WaterQuest water hydraulic motors are approved per Directive 94/9/EC for use in potentially explosive atmospheres, including offshore ATEX zones, grain handling facilities, and confined spaces with ignition risk.
