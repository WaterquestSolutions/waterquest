Performance Validation

Heavy-Duty Performance Curves

Factory-tested performance data for 30cc (1.83 in³) and 35cc (2.14 in³) displacement variants under full-load continuous operation. All measurements conducted with tap water at 20°C (68 °F) ambient temperature.

Output Power (kW)

Peak power output of 25 kW (33.5 hp) achieved with 35cc (2.14 in³) displacement at 160 bar (2,321 psi) and 3000 rpm. Power delivery scales linearly with pressure across full operating envelope.

Overall Efficiency (%)

Maximum overall efficiency of 85.5% at 80 bar (1,160 psi) operating pressure. 35cc (2.14 in³) variant maintains above 82% efficiency from 60-120 bar (870-1,740 psi) at 1500 rpm.

Output Torque (Nm)

Torque output proportional to displacement and system pressure. 35cc (2.14 in³) delivers 84 Nm (62 lbf·ft) at 160 bar (2,321 psi), 30cc (1.83 in³) delivers 71 Nm (52.4 lbf·ft). Ideal for variable-load applications.

Input Flow (L/min)

Flow demand increases slightly with pressure due to internal leakage. 35cc (2.14 in³) at 3000 rpm requires approximately 112 L/min (29.6 US gpm) at rated pressure. Match pump displacement accordingly.

Engineering Features

Built for Extreme Industrial Service

Advanced materials engineering and precision manufacturing for continuous-duty operation in the harshest environments.

Heavy-Duty Stainless Construction

Select the wetted materials and seals for your actual water chemistry and temperature. Send those conditions with the enquiry so the supplied configuration fits the service, including any specific seawater requirements.

Reinforced Shaft Seal System

Select the shaft-seal configuration for pressure, temperature, external exposure and the planned operating cycle. Use the supplier’s service requirements to plan inspection and replacement access.

Industrial Bearing Package

Review the permitted radial and thrust loads for the selected motor. A correctly supported coupling and driven mechanism help keep unintended mechanical loads out of the motor.

Integrated Pressure Relief

Cross-port relief protection must be selected and set for the motor and circuit. Confirm the configuration and allow for pressure spikes during deceleration or external loading so that peak pressure does not exceed 160 bar (2,321 psi) or a lower component limit.

Industrial Applications

Match the motor to the complete operating range

Consider these application types where a water hydraulic drive can address an oil-related problem. Select the motor and supporting circuit against the actual torque, speed and environmental conditions.

Offshore Crane Systems

Winch and slew drives for platform cranes where oil leakage creates environmental penalties and deck contamination hazards.

Underground Mining Conveyors

Belt drive motors in wet underground environments where oil contamination affects ore processing and creates slip hazards.

Wastewater Screening Equipment

Selected screen and compactor drives where removing a hydraulic-oil source beside treatment can benefit the plant.

Desalination RO Trains

High-pressure pump drives and energy recovery turbines in reverse osmosis plants requiring absolute oil-free operation.

Steel Mill Roll Tables

Conveyor drives in hot rolling mills and continuous casting where oil creates fire hazards and product surface defects.

Subsea ROV Thrusters

Propulsion motors for remotely operated vehicles operating at depth where oil leakage creates buoyancy control issues.
