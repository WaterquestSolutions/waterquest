Performance Validation

Heavy-Duty Performance Curves

Factory-tested performance data for 30cc and 35cc displacement variants under full-load continuous operation. All measurements conducted with tap water at 20°C ambient temperature.

Output Power (kW)

Peak power output of 25 kW achieved with 35cc displacement at 160 bar and 3000 rpm. Power delivery scales linearly with pressure across full operating envelope.

Overall Efficiency (%)

Maximum overall efficiency of 85.5% at 80 bar operating pressure. 35cc variant maintains above 82% efficiency from 60-120 bar at 1500 rpm.

Output Torque (Nm)

Torque output proportional to displacement and system pressure. 35cc delivers 84 Nm at 160 bar, 30cc delivers 71 Nm. Ideal for variable-load applications.

Input Flow (L/min)

Flow demand increases slightly with pressure due to internal leakage. 35cc at 3000 rpm requires approximately 112 L/min at rated pressure. Match pump displacement accordingly.

Engineering Features

Built for Extreme Industrial Service

Advanced materials engineering and precision manufacturing for continuous-duty operation in the harshest environments.

Heavy-Duty Stainless Construction

316L stainless steel housing and internal components resist corrosion in seawater, acidic mine water, and industrial process fluids. Carbon-reinforced PEEK sliding surfaces handle high contact pressures without galling.

Reinforced Shaft Seal System

Dual-stage mechanical seal with silicon carbide faces prevents external leakage under continuous operation. Suitable for submerged installations and splash-zone mounting. No shaft seal maintenance required for first 10,000 hours.

Industrial Bearing Package

Oversized ceramic hybrid angular contact bearings handle combined radial and thrust loads. Bearing preload optimized for continuous-duty cycle at rated torque. L10 bearing life exceeds 12,000 hours under maximum radial load.

Integrated Pressure Relief

Internal cross-port relief valve protects motor from pressure spikes during rapid deceleration or external shock loads. Factory-set to 180 bar (12.5% above rated pressure). Field-adjustable with calibrated shim stack.

Mechanical Interface

Mounting and Connections

SAE four-bolt flange with reinforced mounting lugs. Available in MB (integrated brake), MC (coupling flange), and MA (plain shaft) configurations for direct equipment integration.
