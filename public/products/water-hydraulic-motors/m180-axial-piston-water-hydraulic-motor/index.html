M180: water hydraulic rotary power

What it does: Converts pressurized-water flow into rotary torque and speed.

Where it’s used: Rotary mechanisms, drives and water-powered gate arrangements.

Why water: Remove hydraulic oil from the motor circuit with a purpose-designed water motor.

Configuration and operating duty

The range table states 135.0–225 cm³/rev (8.24–13.7 in³/rev), while nominal ordering information includes 134 / 180 / 225 cm³/rev (8.18 / 10.98 / 13.7 in³/rev). The displayed curves and MB drawing cover 180 and 225 cm³/rev variants. The lower-range 134 / 135 discrepancy requires manufacturer confirmation; neither value is automatically substituted into the 180 or 225 curves. Select the exact displacement, return-pressure and case-drain arrangement with the quotation.

Engineering Notes

Built for water hydraulic operation.

Large-displacement output

The published range is 135.0–225 cm³/rev (8.24–13.7 in³/rev). The curves and dimensional sheet on this page cover the 180 and 225 cm³/rev (10.98 and 13.7 in³/rev) variants; they do not provide a separate 135 cm³/rev performance selection.

Tap-water operation

Built for clean hydraulic systems where water is the working medium and petroleum leakage is unacceptable.

Boosted motor configuration

The supplied MB drawing covers MB160-180W and MB160-225W. Confirm return pressure relative to the separate case drain, seal type and installation requirements on the selected order drawing.

Heavy industrial fit

Appropriate for high-load equipment in marine, infrastructure, desalination, and industrial environments.
