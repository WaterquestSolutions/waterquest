In focus: Duplex 2205 seawater pump construction

The Duplex seawater pump uses seawater itself for cooling and lubrication. Its water-contacting components are machined from Duplex 2205 bar stock, giving the pump a construction selected specifically for seawater service.

Why Duplex seawater service

Water-lubricated pumping. Materials selected for seawater.

Seawater pumps use advanced materials and loaded sliding surfaces to operate with seawater as the only coolant and lubricant. The Duplex build uses bar-stock Duplex 2205 for all water-contacting components, with no cast parts.

No oil contamination

Water lubrication removes lubricating oil from this pump’s wetted path, reducing one contamination source.

80 bar (1,160 psi) continuous

Control nominal flow through shaft speed; use the curves to account for leakage and efficiency.

Three-port boosted

Separate feed, discharge, and case-drain ports isolate leakage flow and reduce seal loading.

Design and ordering

Designed for pressurized seawater feed.

Inlet and case drain

Maintain constant positive inlet pressure. As a guide, P6 to P300 models require approximately 3.0 to 3.6 bar (43.5 to 52.2 psi) minimum inlet pressure; P600 requires approximately 3.5 to 4.1 bar (50.8 to 59.5 psi). Inlet pressure must remain at least 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) above casing pressure.

Porting

  • A: low-pressure inlet
  • B: high-pressure outlet
  • C: tank return line to a low-pressure drain or sump

Filtration and flushing

Incoming seawater must be filtered to an absolute 10 micron rating with a Beta Ratio greater than 5000. Flush the system with permeate whenever it is taken out of operation and establish a weekly fresh-water flushing regime where practical.

Rotation

All seawater pumps are designed for anticlockwise operation unless specifically requested otherwise, viewed from the pump shaft.

PB80-70DMS

Example ordering code: boosted pump, 80 bar (1,160 psi) maximum operating pressure, 70 cc/rev (4.27 in³/rev) displacement, Duplex build, mechanical seal. The fluid, duty cycle, temperature, chloride concentration, and seal requirement should be confirmed on every enquiry.
