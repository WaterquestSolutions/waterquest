Axial piston fire-system pumps

What it does: Supplies high-pressure water for the selected fire-protection pumping duty.

Where it’s used: Water-mist systems designed and reviewed by the responsible fire-protection designer.

Why water: Provide a water-service pump selected for the complete system duty and approval route.

When fire protection is needed, the system must perform. There is no second opportunity.

These pumps supply high-pressure water to water-mist systems selected and reviewed by the responsible fire-protection designer. We support pump selection; the system designer establishes the complete system’s listing and approval requirements.

Define the fire-system duty

Start with the approved suppression design and the simultaneous flow and pressure required at its supply point. The pump must fit that complete duty.

Plan commissioning and continuing service

Specify acceptance measurements, protective functions and inspection access. Confirm water quality, priming, standby conditions and restart requirements. The selected equipment must remain testable over the facility’s service life.
