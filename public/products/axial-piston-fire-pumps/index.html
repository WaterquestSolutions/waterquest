Frequently Asked Questions

WaterQuest Solutions Fire Pump
Technical FAQ

An axial piston fire pump uses multiple pistons arranged axially around a rotating shaft to generate high pressure water flow. The WaterQuest Solutions design uses nine pistons specifically to deliver smooth, ripple-free pressure up to 160 bar. The nine-piston configuration eliminates the pressure pulses that single or triple-piston designs produce, removing the need for accumulators or pulsation dampers in the suppression system.

Oil contamination in a fire suppression system creates multiple risks: it can compromise the firefighting water, coat and clog nozzles reducing atomization efficiency, and introduce a secondary flammable fluid near heat and ignition sources. The WaterQuest Solutions pump uses water as its only coolant and lubricant, meaning any internal fluid that reaches the suppression circuit is simply clean water. This is particularly critical in healthcare, food processing, and hazardous area applications.

WaterQuest Solutions axial piston fire pumps are ATEX certified to Directive 94/9/EC, Category II 2GD ck T6 T85 degrees Celsius, suitable for Zone 1 gas/vapor and Zone 21 dust hazardous environments. This covers oil refineries, chemical processing plants, offshore platforms, grain handling facilities, and any confined space where ignition risk from vaporized oil cannot be accepted.

The P6 has a 6.0 cc/rev displacement and is suited to medium-scale systems and light industrial fire suppression. The P60 has a 60.0 cc/rev displacement, delivering ten times the flow volume, making it suitable for large industrial plants, offshore platforms, and major structures. Both operate at the same 160 bar maximum pressure. The P1 through P180 range allows a single pump to serve applications from cabinet-scale protection right through to multi-building campus systems.
