Define the fire-system duty

Start with the approved suppression design and the simultaneous flow and pressure required at its supply point. Include the loss through pipework and controls, emergency power and the required operating duration. The pump must fit that complete duty.

Confirm the approval boundary

A pump component rating is not a listing for a complete water-mist or sprinkler system. Obtain the documentation required by the responsible fire-protection designer and authority having jurisdiction. Hazardous-area suitability also needs current evidence for the exact equipment configuration.

Plan commissioning and continuing service

Specify acceptance measurements, protective functions and inspection access. Confirm water quality, priming, standby conditions and restart requirements. The selected equipment must remain testable over the facility’s service life.
