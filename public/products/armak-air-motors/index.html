One coordinated drive

Build the package around the work.

From air pressure to shaft torque

A power piston transfers torque through a synchronized gear train and a second, non-contacting rotating piston. The modular arrangement connects the motor to its output equipment, control valve and accessories.

IEC B5 mounting, keyed or internally splined outputs, and replacement interface variants give different routes into an existing mechanism. Check the actual shaft, flange and load dimensions before choosing an interface.

Control at the machine

Lever control provides variable operation at the mechanism. Confirm handle position and the required high-power direction.

Control from a station

Remote proportional valves let the control station suit the operator and installation. Match the valve and air paths to the motor.

Manage the load

Specify brakes, exhaust control and stopping behaviour together. A back-driving load needs a defined lowering and holding arrangement.

Define the complete duty

Selection notes.

Air supply and operating limits

Maximum inlet pressure: 8 bar (116 psi). Published comparison data above use 6 bar (87 psi) at the motor. Allow for pressure loss through pipework, fittings, the filter/regulator/lubricator, valves and silencers.

Operating temperature: −10 to +80°C (14 to 176°F). Maximum inlet-air temperature: 65°C (149°F). Model sheets specify oil drip-feed lubrication of 6–10 drops/min for short runs and 3–4 drops/min for continuous operation. Confirm lubricant and service requirements in the current manual.

Starting, continuous running and stopping

Check starting torque separately from running torque. The brochure recommends using 60% of dynamic torque for starting-under-load calculations; agree the starting basis for the actual duty instead of assuming the tabulated starting value covers every condition.

Allow for gearbox efficiency, stall loading and inertia. Limit off-load speed. For back-driving duties, coordinate the proportional valve, check plate and brake release pressure; the brake is intended as a static holding device in the described arrangement.

Direction, interfaces and hazardous areas

State rotation as viewed on the shaft and identify which direction needs maximum power. Some power-valve arrangements have different performance in reverse. Specify shaft, flange, radial/axial loads, mounting orientation, control position and any gearbox or brake.

The brochure lists ATEX versions as an option. Request the certificate and operating conditions for the exact ordered assembly, gas/dust group and location; an air motor alone does not establish approval for a complete installation.
