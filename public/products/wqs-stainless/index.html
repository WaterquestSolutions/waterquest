Material Advantage

Why 316 Stainless?
Why It Matters.

1

Superior Corrosion Resistance

316 SS contains molybdenum, providing exceptional protection against chlorides and industrial chemicals that outperforms standard 304 steel. The material of choice for marine, chemical, and food environments.

2

High Strength Under Pressure

Maintains structural integrity in high-pressure and high-temperature environments. Critical for hydraulic and gas systems where failure is not an option and pressure ratings must be sustained long-term.

3

Performance at Temperature Extremes

Performs reliably from cryogenic liquid nitrogen temperatures to high-heat refinery processes without becoming brittle. Ideal for applications spanning cold storage, LNG, and high-temperature processing.

4

Hygienic and Non-Reactive

Smooth, non-porous surface is easy to clean and does not react with food, pharmaceuticals, or biological materials. FDA and NSF compliant for direct contact applications in regulated industries.

Frequently Asked Questions

316 SS Fitting
Technical FAQ

316 stainless steel contains 2 to 3% molybdenum, which dramatically improves resistance to chloride-induced pitting and crevice corrosion. This makes 316 SS the correct choice wherever exposure to seawater, brine, acidic chemicals, bleach-based cleaning agents, or marine atmospheres is possible. 304 lacks molybdenum and will corrode in these environments over time, compromising seal integrity and fitting life.

WQS Stainless products meet or exceed ISO, SAE, API, FDA, NSF, ASME, and USP international standards, ensuring suitability for worldwide applications.
