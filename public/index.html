How water hydraulics works

Pressure creates force. Flow creates movement.

A driven pump sends water through valves to a cylinder or motor. The drive supplies the energy.

Read the circuit explanation

01 / Pump

Supply the flow.

The pump moves water; load resistance develops pressure.

02 / Valves

Control the response.

Valves control direction, speed and stopping.

03 / Cylinders

Lift, push and pull.

Cylinders produce linear force and movement.

04 / Motors

Turn the load.

Motors produce rotary torque and movement.
