Up to 40% · CAD $500,000 maximum

For incorporated BC SMEs demonstrating a technology with a BC pilot partner. The listed call started at technology readiness level (TRL) 6 or above and requires progress of at least one level.

BC Hydro · Feasibility studies

Up to 100% of an approved study

A starting point for eligible industrial and large commercial customers investigating measurable electricity savings, electrification or demand response.

Fit, conditions and next step

Customer and minimum savings thresholds apply. Submit the proposal and obtain approval before the study begins. Establish the existing duty and electrical input: removing hydraulic oil alone does not demonstrate an energy saving.

FortisBC · Custom Efficiency

Industrial incentive cap: CAD $1.5 million

For eligible FortisBC gas customers saving gas, or eligible FortisBC and participating municipal electricity customers saving electricity. Custom projects need an approved energy study.

Fit, conditions and next step

Study support starts at up to 50%. Implementation support is limited by calculated lifetime energy savings, 75% of invoiced costs and the applicable customer cap; commercial projects have a lower cap. Confirm utility territory and the approved consultant before starting.

Contribution assessed for the project

For incorporated, for-profit Canadian SMEs with up to 500 full-time equivalent employees developing and commercializing innovative technology.

15% base rate · 35% for qualifying claimants

Supports qualifying research and experimental development expenditure. Certain eligible corporations can access the enhanced rate within a CAD $6 million expenditure limit for tax years beginning after 15 December 2024.

Make the funding plan workable

Put approvals ahead
of purchase orders.

Agree who owns the project and who pays each cost. A supplier, host business, municipality and research institution may have very different funding routes.

Discuss the pilot brief →

  1. Confirm the applicant and activity

    Match legal entity, location, project stage, technical work and eligible costs to the program.

  2. Obtain the required written approval

    Many incentives exclude costs committed before approval or an agreement. Check the specific rule before signing.

  3. Build one transparent funding table

    Declare every contribution. Check stacking limits and have a tax adviser calculate the remaining credit base.

  4. Plan the cash flow and evidence

    Allow for reimbursement delays, matching funds, invoices, technical records and measurement reports.
