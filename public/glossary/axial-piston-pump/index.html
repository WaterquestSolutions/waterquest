Axial piston pump

An axial piston pump is a positive displacement pump that uses pistons arranged parallel to the drive shaft to generate hydraulic flow.

ComponentsTerm

Short technical definition with WaterQuest product and knowledge links.

What it means

An axial piston pump is a positive displacement pump that uses pistons arranged parallel to the drive shaft to generate hydraulic flow.

Why it matters in water hydraulics

In WaterQuest systems, axial piston pump architecture is adapted for water by using corrosion-resistant wetted materials, tight clearances, and water-compatible sliding interfaces.

Practical example

Axial piston pumps are used where compact high-pressure flow is needed for cylinders, motors, fire pumps, and clean hydraulic power units.

Useful formula or design note

Flow is driven by displacement per revolution multiplied by shaft speed, adjusted for volumetric efficiency.
