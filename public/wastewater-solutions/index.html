Platform Overview

Hydraulic power, controls, data, and documentation designed as one system.

Bring water hydraulic equipment, selected monitoring and project information into a coordinated scope. Define the modules your plant needs, the interfaces to existing controls and the evidence required at commissioning. Monitoring and digital functions are specified for the installation.

The procurement advantage is a clearer responsibility boundary: engineered hydraulic packages, water-compatible materials, controls integration, FAT/SAT procedures, commissioning records, operator training, and lifecycle support can be specified as part of one coherent scope.

Engineering and Procurement Priorities

Define operating pressure, flow, duty cycle, water quality, environment, controls, documentation, and maintenance requirements before component selection.

Keep WaterQuest hydraulic equipment at or below 160 bar (2,321 psi) (2,321 PSI) and use 316 stainless, PEEK/PTFE, and water-compatible wetted materials.

Specify FAT, SAT, hydrostatic testing, commissioning records, O&M manuals, spare parts, and operator training as deliverables, not optional extras.
