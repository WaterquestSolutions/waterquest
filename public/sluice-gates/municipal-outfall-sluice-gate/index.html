Project Example / Municipal

Control the outfall with water hydraulic actuation.

Keep the outfall working while removing an oil source above receiving water. Build the water hydraulic actuation around drainage duty, reverse-flow control and the gate’s required response to lost power, with a practical recovery plan for operators.

Discuss Similar ProjectShop Components

Problem

High consequence location, limited tolerance for leaks.

An outfall gate works close to receiving water, where an oil leak can add clean-up and interruption to an equipment fault. Water hydraulic actuation removes that working-oil source. Size the movement for the head difference and gate resistance, then coordinate storm operation and maintenance access.

Specification angle

A useful tender connects the oil-removal benefit to a gate that performs its required stroke and force. Include controls, service access and acceptance measurements so bidders can propose a complete supported package.

Recommended scope

Stainless water hydraulic cylinder sized to gate head pressure, friction, and stroke.

Local HPU with filtration, relief protection, pressure indication, and drain/flush points.

PLC status for open, closed, fault, pressure, run command, and maintenance lockout.

316 stainless fittings, tube supports, isolation valves, and service access details.

Owner Benefits

Better failure mode, cleaner operations.
