Why Water Hydraulics

Use the same medium the structure already manages.

Gates often sit directly beside the water they control. Removing hydraulic oil from their actuation circuit removes an oil source from hoses, fittings and seals near that environment. Pair that benefit with the required gate movement and accessible service points to make the upgrade useful to operators.

Environmental ExposureNo petroleum fluid above the waterwayA fitting weep, damaged hose, or seal leak does not release hydraulic oil into the channel, lagoon, intake, or receiving water.
OperationsCleaner maintenance workService crews focus on filters, seals, valves, rod condition, and water quality rather than absorbents, spill kits, oil disposal, and cleanup reporting.

Engineering Basis

Design starts with gate load, not actuator product range size.

A properly sized WaterGate package begins with the civil and hydraulic load case: gate width and height, differential head, seal drag, guide friction, debris loading, corrosion allowance, silt, ice, required travel speed, and fail-position requirement. Those inputs determine cylinder bore, rod size, stroke, pump flow, relief setting, controls, and tubing size.

For retrofit work, WaterQuest also reviews the existing gate frame, guide wear, mounting structure, access envelope, electrical service, PLC interface, and maintenance isolation requirements. This avoids the common mistake of installing a strong actuator on a gate that binds mechanically.

Gate actuator: stainless cylinder or rotary water hydraulic drive selected for gate force, stroke, mounting geometry, travel speed, holding duty, and service access.

Oil vs Water Risk

The biggest advantage is what happens when something goes wrong.

Water hydraulic actuation changes the fluid that could escape at the gate. Keep inspection and maintenance focused on water quality, seals, tubing and mechanical condition so the system continues to deliver the required movement.

Materials and Water Quality

Water-compatible materials are the system, not an accessory.

Water has different lubricity, corrosion behaviour, and biological risk than oil. WaterQuest systems are built around stainless wetted components, compatible polymers, fine filtration, and careful surface pairing. Where two sliding or rotating surfaces contact, one is selected as 316 stainless and the other as carbon-reinforced PEEK where applicable.

316 SS

Used for fittings, manifolds, tubes, wetted hardware, and exposed components where corrosion resistance is essential.

PEEK/PTFE

Used in compatible bearing, guide, seal, and sliding interfaces depending on load and motion.

10 µm (394 µin)

Fine filtration target protects valves, pumps, actuators, and control orifices from abrasive solids.

MTR

Material certification, drawings, test records, and spare parts lists can be included for project documentation.
