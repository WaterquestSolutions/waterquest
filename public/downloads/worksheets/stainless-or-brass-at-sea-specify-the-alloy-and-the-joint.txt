REVIEW THE TOPIC
Use these guide sections to check the entries above. Record unresolved items; do not turn an unknown value into an assumed rating.
- Make every connection part of the marine design
- Alloy names matter at a marine joint
- Identify the actual alloy and product form
- Distinguish salt spray from continuous seawater contact
- Understand local corrosion in stainless connections
- Recognize that brasses differ in seawater behaviour
- Evaluate dissimilar-metal connections as a system
- Match the connection standard and sealing method
- Control mechanical loads beyond internal pressure
- Design inspection and replacement into the installation
- Choose from the complete service conditions
- Review the complete marine connection
