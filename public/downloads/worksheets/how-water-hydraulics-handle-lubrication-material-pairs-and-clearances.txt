REVIEW THE TOPIC
Use these guide sections to check the entries above. Record unresolved items; do not turn an unknown value into an assumed rating.
- Design the moving contacts to work with water
- Pressure transmission and lubrication are different functions
- Think in terms of two surfaces and their motion
- Understand what a fluid film contributes
- Choose polymer grades for the function they perform
- Balance clearance against leakage and contact behaviour
- Distinguish friction, wear and leakage in testing
- Cleanliness protects an engineered contact
- Validate representative starts, stops and operating conditions
- Describe progress without claiming that limits vanished
- Turn lubrication challenges into interface requirements
- Turn this guidance into a project decision
