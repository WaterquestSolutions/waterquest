REVIEW THE TOPIC
Use these guide sections to check the entries above. Record unresolved items; do not turn an unknown value into an assumed rating.
- Choose valve functions that make the sequence work
- Directional valves define the available flow paths
- Relief and reducing valves answer different pressure questions
- Flow control influences speed under defined conditions
- Check valves and holding functions require context
- Return flow can govern the selected passage
- Actuation and control electronics are part of the valve
- Water compatibility includes hidden details
- Verify the sequence rather than testing one position
- Ask for a circuit explanation you can follow
- Select the valve at its intended pressure drop
- Turn this guidance into a project decision
