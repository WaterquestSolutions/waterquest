REVIEW THE TOPIC
Use these guide sections to check the entries above. Record unresolved items; do not turn an unknown value into an assumed rating.
- Build around the water supply your site needs
- Reverse osmosis uses pressure to make a separation
- Follow three streams rather than one output number
- Saline service requires a specific construction review
- Pretreatment protects the process and its machinery
- An inlet problem can undermine an otherwise suitable pump
- Pressure energy in concentrate can have useful value
- Measure energy per useful quantity of water
- Drought readiness includes interruptions and recovery
- Concentrate management belongs in the project from the start
- Procure a performance envelope and a service plan
- Build the case around dependable water, not a component slogan
