REVIEW THE TOPIC
Use these guide sections to check the entries above. Record unresolved items; do not turn an unknown value into an assumed rating.
- Compare efficiency where your pump will run
- Establish theoretical and delivered flow
- Calculate hydraulic output from the pressure difference
- Add the electrical boundary when comparing power units
- Compare a useful operating map, not one attractive point
- Include the energy consumed while the actuator is waiting
- Understand the role of viscosity without oversimplifying it
- Design the test to separate improvements from uncertainty
- Put efficiency beside the other reasons for selecting water
- Compare efficiency at matched duty points
