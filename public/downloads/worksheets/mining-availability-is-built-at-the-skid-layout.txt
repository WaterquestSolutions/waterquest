Use these guide sections to check the entries above. Record unresolved items; do not turn an unknown value into an assumed rating.

- Define what availability means for the duty
- Identify the oil-exposure problem precisely
- Treat mine water as a variable input
- Design service access before finalizing the skid
- Build a spare-parts plan around actual configurations
- Keep hydraulic actuation separate from spray performance
- Plan for contamination outside the circuit
- Make fault response and energy isolation explicit
- Use a pilot to measure both production and maintenance
- Measure availability at the circuit boundary
