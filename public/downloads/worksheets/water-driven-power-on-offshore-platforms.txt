REVIEW THE TOPIC
Use these guide sections to check the entries above. Record unresolved items; do not turn an unknown value into an assumed rating.
- Design for the sea outside and the water inside
- Hazardous-area and offshore approvals
- Choose a candidate function with a clear boundary
- Turn load descriptions into operating states
- Do not assume seawater is the obvious working medium
- Corrosion control extends beyond a stainless label
- Hydraulic power still depends on the complete path
- Define the response to loss of a shared service
- Stored energy must remain visible during maintenance
- Service logistics can dominate the ownership experience
- Test representative conditions before relying on the package
- Keep environmental and hazardous-area claims precise
