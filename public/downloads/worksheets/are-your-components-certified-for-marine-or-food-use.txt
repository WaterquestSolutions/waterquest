REVIEW THE TOPIC
Use these guide sections to check the entries above. Record unresolved items; do not turn an unknown value into an assumed rating.
- Get the evidence that fits your application
- Three different kinds of approval evidence
- Distinguish material evidence from equipment approval
- Define the marine function and vessel context
- Read approval scope rather than relying on a logo
- Identify the food-process exposure pathway
- Do not equate potable supply with sanitary machinery
- Use a requirements matrix to expose gaps
- Keep component and system responsibilities explicit
- Preserve approval status when the configuration changes
- Ask for evidence that supports a clear answer
- Match documents to the supplied assembly
