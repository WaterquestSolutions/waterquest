REVIEW THE TOPIC
Use these guide sections to check the entries above. Record unresolved items; do not turn an unknown value into an assumed rating.
- Give every valve a clear job
- Read the symbol as a map of possible paths
- Match the internal design to water service
- Understand flow capacity at a stated pressure difference
- Size supply and return paths for their actual flows
- Distinguish metering from load control
- Separate pressure regulation from protective limitation
- Check how the valve is actuated
- Evaluate leakage in relation to the required hold
- Validate response, transitions and the final arrangement
- Match metering behaviour to the load
- Turn this guidance into a project decision
