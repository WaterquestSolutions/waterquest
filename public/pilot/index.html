A pilot ends with a decision

Example pilot: one gate actuator

Replace one oil-hydraulic gate actuator with a water unit and compare three months of operation with the recorded baseline. Measure stroke time, pressure, cycles, leakage observations and maintenance hours at equivalent loads.

Illustrative decision criteria: meet the owner’s agreed stroke-time range in both directions; complete the agreed operating and fault-response tests; remain within the selected component limits; and meet the agreed maintenance-hours target. Set numerical thresholds, stop criteria, responsibilities and approval gates before installation.

Start at the scale that answers your question: a feasibility review, a controlled trial or an instrumented installation. Each step should make the next investment decision clearer.

Feasibility & baseline

Validated rollout

Use the pilot findings to refine the design, operating procedures, service plan and whole-life cost before expanding the installation.

Decision outputA documented case for the next investment.

Scope, price, schedule, equipment ownership and support are agreed in writing. A pilot enquiry is not a purchase commitment or a funding application.

Spend less where the evidence supports it

Build savings into the design.

Compare systems at equivalent useful output. Count both avoided costs and any new costs of water treatment, materials, filtration and service.

01

Right-size the circuit

Match pump, actuator and control strategy to the duty. Measure loaded and standby energy use; avoid paying for unnecessary capacity or continuous operation.

02

Cost the fluid change

Compare hydraulic oil purchases, oil changes and waste handling with the proposed water supply, treatment, filtration and inspection requirements.

03

Make service measurable

Record labour, consumables, planned service and production interruptions. Use observed intervals and site costs rather than assumed maintenance savings.

04

Keep the first scope focused

Choose one representative duty and reuse suitable test equipment where practical. Separate essential measurements from optional features.

05

Plan around the shutdown

Coordinate installation, access and operator training with scheduled work. Include commissioning, spares and the cost of restoring the original arrangement.

06

Separate risk from cash savings

Removing hydraulic oil from the circuit changes one source of leakage risk. Keep avoided incident scenarios separate from recurring, demonstrable operating savings.

Make the base case work first.

Pilot questions

Know what happens next.

A useful trial is a shared plan with clear responsibilities, costs and decision points.

Do you offer a free or fully funded pilot?

There is no automatic free-pilot entitlement. We first review the application and then agree the scope, equipment, engineering support, price and responsibilities. Any third-party funding is subject to that provider’s assessment and written terms.

Which funding route should I try first?

Start with the applicant and the work. An established site saving electricity may fit a utility incentive; a developer resolving technical uncertainty may fit innovation or research support; a community infrastructure project needs the municipal or First Nation applicant and its planning process.

Can I combine a grant and tax credits?

Possibly, but stacking limits and assistance adjustments apply. Disclose all support to each funder and ask your tax adviser to calculate the net credit on the remaining qualifying expenditure. Do not simply add the published rates.

Will water hydraulics use less energy?

It depends on the complete system and operating duty. Compare electrical input at equivalent useful output, including standby, control losses, water treatment and auxiliaries. The pilot should establish the result rather than assume it.

What information helps define a pilot?

Tell us what moves or what process needs pressure, where it operates, the present equipment, the duty cycle and the problem you want to solve.

Can work start while a funding application is under review?

Only if you understand and accept the program’s eligibility rules. Many funders exclude earlier commitments or costs. Obtain the required written approval before ordering, contracting or starting work you expect the program to support.
