The Case for Change

Why Switch to
Water Hydraulics?

⚠️

The Problem with Oil Hydraulics

Spill Risk & Contamination Oil leaks near waterways, food systems, or municipal infrastructure trigger costly cleanups and regulatory action.
Compliance Complexity Ongoing monitoring, containment systems, and incident reporting add overhead to every project.
High Lifecycle Cost Hydraulic oil replacement, filter disposal, and downtime from seal failures accumulate over time.
Regulatory Trajectory Tightening federal and provincial rules are making oil-based systems increasingly difficult to permit.
💧

The WaterQuest Advantage

Runs on Tap or Seawater No additives, no hazardous fluid handling, just clean water as the working fluid. No spill risk, period.
Zero Oil Spill Risk Eliminates spill liability, containment requirements, and associated insurance costs entirely.
Lower Lifecycle Cost Fewer consumables, simpler maintenance, and longer service life in corrosive environments.
Simpler Compliance & Reporting 316L SS + PEEK wetted pairs meet stringent environmental and food-grade standards out of the box.

R&D tax credit for prototyping, integration, and custom engineering work. Federal (35%) + BC provincial (10%) combined maximum return on qualifying R&D.
