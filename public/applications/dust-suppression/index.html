APPLICATION

Dust Suppression Systems

Water hydraulic systems power high-pressure misting and dust suppression equipment for mining operations, bulk material handling, demolition sites, and construction projects. Dust suppression requires reliable high-pressure pumping without petroleum contamination risk in applications where suppression water discharge enters natural waterways or stormwater systems.

Dust Suppression Applications

Dust suppression systems atomise water into fine mist for airborne particulate control in mining, material handling, and construction operations. High-pressure water delivery creates droplet sizes effective for dust capture while minimising water consumption and ground surface saturation. Effective dust suppression requires system pressures from 800 to 3,000 psi depending on nozzle configuration and coverage requirements.

Dust suppression equipment operates in abrasive environments with high particulate loading. Water hydraulic pumps eliminate petroleum contamination concern in applications where hydraulic fluid mixing with suppression water would create environmental violations. Mining operations discharging suppression water to fish-bearing streams face strict petroleum prohibition under federal and provincial environmental regulations. Water hydraulic systems eliminate this compliance risk entirely.

System Design Considerations

High-Pressure Delivery Requirements

Dust suppression effectiveness depends on droplet size distribution, which requires system pressures from 800 to 3,000 psi depending on nozzle configuration. Water hydraulic pumps deliver these pressures with flow rates from 5 to 100 gallons per minute for comprehensive site coverage. Higher pressures produce finer droplet atomisation for respirable dust capture, while lower pressures provide coarser spray for heavier particulate settling.

Material Requirements for Water Hydraulic Service

All water hydraulic components require corrosion-resistant materials at internal contact surfaces. WaterQuest gate actuation cylinders use 316 stainless steel for cylinder barrels, piston rods, end caps, port connections, and mounting hardware. Internal sealing surfaces employ carbon-reinforced PEEK, which provides wear resistance and low friction characteristics without requiring petroleum-based lubrication additives.

Abrasive Environment Operation

Dust suppression equipment operates in abrasive environments with airborne particulate concentrations that accelerate component wear through erosive attack on pump internals, valve surfaces, and cylinder seals. Water hydraulic pumps benefit from simplified maintenance as system flushing uses the same water supply as the suppression application, eliminating disposal requirements for contaminated hydraulic oil. All-stainless-steel construction provides superior wear resistance compared to conventional cast iron or aluminium hydraulic components.
