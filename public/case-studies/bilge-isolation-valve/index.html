Home / Case studies / Bilge isolation valve

Marine · Equipment supply

Remove the oil.
Keep the isolation duty.

A cruise ship’s oil-actuated bilge valve leaked hydraulic oil into the bilge tank. A water-actuated globe valve removes the actuator’s hydraulic-oil source from that leak path.

Discuss a similar valve
Application
Marine bilge isolation
Valve
DN200 PN6 globe valve
Opening
Water hand pump
Closing
Spring return*

The challenge

An actuator leak reached the bilge tank.

The existing valve used hydraulic oil for actuation. Leakage introduced that oil into the bilge tank, creating a contamination and cleanup problem at equipment intended to isolate the bilge system.

The replacement valve

A water-actuated replacement valve.

A water-actuated DN200 PN6 globe isolation valve provides the replacement. A hand pump provides the pressure to open the valve. Spring force returns it closed when opening pressure is released through the designed return path.

The change

Remove hydraulic oil from this leak path.

Using water for actuation removes the actuator’s hydraulic-oil source from the identified leak path. This equipment-supply case does not report measured operating hours, cost savings or vessel-wide performance.

*Spring return requires release of actuator pressure. Trapped opening pressure can hold the valve open; stopping or isolating the pump alone does not establish closure.
