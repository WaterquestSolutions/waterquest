Six Reasons

Why Water Hydraulics Fit
Every Municipal Application

🌊

Zero Oil in Critical Water Systems

No hydrocarbon leaks, no oil sheen, no cleanup costs, no containment systems. Water hydraulics remove contamination risk entirely from the facilities built to protect water quality.

⚙️

Reliability in Corrosive Moisture Environments

316 SS and PEEK resist H₂S, chlorine, humidity, buried vaults, and standing water. These environments destroy oil systems, they're what water hydraulics are engineered for.

🔧

Much Lower Maintenance Requirements

No oil changes, filter replacements, fluid sampling, hazardous disposal, or spill traceback. Lean municipal crews stay focused on critical infrastructure tasks, not oil management.

💪

Force & Precision for Large Gates and Valves

Multi-ton gates, large-diameter valves, and long-stroke movement all need consistent high-pressure force. Water delivers it where air can't, and oil falters in cold weather.

🔥

No Fire or Ignition Risk in Confined Spaces

Pump stations, electrical rooms, generator halls, and underground vaults carry real ignition risk with oil. Water removes it entirely, no vapours, no flash point, no fuel source.

🌡️

Natural Temperature Stability Year-Round

Oil changes viscosity in cold Canadian winters causing slow gates, pressure spikes, and pump strain. Water stays stable across freeze-thaw cycles, providing predictable year-round performance.

Emergency Reliability No Warm-Up, No Lag

Storm events, sewer backups, and pump failures demand instant response. Water hydraulics perform consistently at -40°C with no thickening, no pressure drop from heat, and no performance lag when communities need it most.

🌱

Longer Life in the Harshest Municipal Environments

316 SS and PEEK eliminate corrosion from H₂S, chlorine, humidity, and standing water. No oil to degrade, no filters to change, systems last longer and require fewer replacements, supporting sustainable long-term infrastructure.
