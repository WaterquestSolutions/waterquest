Hydrogen Production

Oil-Free Hydraulics for Hydrogen Production Infrastructure

Hydrogen production facilities manage flammable gas, high-pressure systems, and water as a process input. Petroleum hydraulic oil has no place in that environment. Water hydraulic systems use water as the hydraulic medium: non-flammable, process-compatible, and already present in every electrolysis facility.

The Safety Case is Non-Negotiable

Petroleum hydraulic oil has a flash point of approximately 200°C and an autoignition temperature around 340°C. In facilities producing or handling hydrogen, a pressurized oil mist from a ruptured hydraulic line in the presence of an ignition source represents a catastrophic fire and explosion risk. Water hydraulic systems remove this risk category entirely. A ruptured water hydraulic line releases water.

The Problem with Oil in Hydrogen Facilities

Oil Hydraulics Introduce Avoidable Risk Into Clean Energy Infrastructure

Hydrogen production plants require strict safety controls due to the flammable nature of hydrogen gas. Traditional hydraulic systems using petroleum oil can introduce unnecessary hazards into an environment already requiring rigorous management of combustible materials.

For infrastructure designed to produce clean energy, the contradiction of using a petroleum-based fluid as a hydraulic medium deserves direct engineering attention.

🔥

Flammable hydraulic fluid co-located with hydrogen gas systems creates compounding explosion risk

🔥

Oil leaks creating contamination risks in process water systems and electrolysis infrastructure

🔥

Maintenance requirements for hazardous petroleum fluid handling in safety-critical environments

🔥

Fire risks compounded by the presence of both combustible gas and combustible hydraulic fluid

🔥

Environmental and ESG reporting exposure from petroleum fluid management at a clean energy facility

What Water Hydraulics Resolve

Water is non-flammable. A hydraulic line rupture releases water, not a combustible mist. The fire and explosion risk from the hydraulic system is eliminated by design.

No petroleum contamination of process water systems. In electrolysis facilities where water purity is critical to electrolyzer performance, this matters operationally as well as environmentally.

316 stainless steel and PEEK materials require no petroleum fluid inventory, no oil analysis programme, and no hazardous waste disposal logistics.

Water hydraulic systems deliver strong, reliable actuation at up to 160 bar for isolation valves, emergency shutdown systems, and process control throughout the facility.

Clean energy credentials are reinforced by clean hydraulic infrastructure. Petroleum-free operation is consistent with the environmental positioning of green hydrogen production.

⚡

The Electrolysis Synergy

Electrolysis-based hydrogen production uses water as its raw material. In a water hydraulic system, the working fluid and the process input are the same substance. This means the hydraulic system can draw from the same treated water supply as the electrolyzer, simplifying infrastructure and eliminating a separate fluid management stream entirely.

Advantages

Why Water Hydraulics Belong in Hydrogen Infrastructure

🚫🔥

Non-Flammable Hydraulic Medium

Water does not ignite. In facilities where hydrogen gas may be present, removing every combustible material that is not operationally necessary is a basic safety principle. Water hydraulics remove the combustible hydraulic fluid from the risk equation without compromising actuation performance.

🌱

Clean Energy Infrastructure Alignment

Green hydrogen production is built around the argument that clean energy requires clean processes. Operating petroleum hydraulic systems inside a green hydrogen facility is an inconsistency that water hydraulic technology resolves. Zero oil means zero hydrocarbon footprint in the hydraulic system.

🔒

Reliable Safety-Critical Valve Actuation

Isolation valves and emergency shutdown systems in hydrogen plants must actuate reliably under normal and emergency conditions. Water hydraulic actuators at up to 160 bar provide the force and repeatability required for safety-critical applications, with pilot-operated check valves maintaining load-holding without a running pump.

🔧

Reduced Maintenance Requirements

Water hydraulic systems eliminate oil filtration equipment, fluid replacement schedules, oil analysis programmes, and hazardous waste disposal contracts. In hydrogen facilities where maintenance access may be restricted by safety protocols, reducing hydraulic maintenance complexity is a direct operational benefit.

💧

Process Fluid Compatibility

In electrolysis-based hydrogen production, the hydraulic working fluid (water) and the process input (water) are the same substance. The hydraulic system can draw from the same treated water supply as the electrolyzer, simplifying facility water management and eliminating separate hydraulic fluid procurement and storage.

📊

ESG and Regulatory Positioning

Green hydrogen projects are subject to increasing scrutiny from project financiers, insurers, and regulators regarding environmental performance. Operating petroleum-free hydraulic systems removes a potential ESG flag and simplifies environmental reporting for facilities committed to zero-hydrocarbon infrastructure.
