Why Water Hydraulics Fit
Every Mining Application

🌊

Zero Oil Contamination

Water-only circuits protect groundwater, ore streams, sumps, and haulage areas. No environmental reporting required for any leak, the medium is simply water.

🔥

Fire Elimination Underground

No flammable vapour, mist, or oil film residue. Critical for underground mines, coal operations, enclosed facilities, and any location where ignition sources must be eliminated.

💪

Strong, Stable Force Output

Consistent force, precise control, and repeatable actuation across long continuous cycles. No heat fade, no viscosity drift, no pressure loss, full power when the mine needs it.

🌡️

Excellent Heat Management

Water naturally manages heat. Improves component life, valve stability, pump efficiency, and actuator precision near crushers, mills, and other high-heat processing equipment.

🔧

Much Lower Maintenance

No oil changes, filter swaps, varnish buildup, oil sampling, or spill cleanup. Systems run longer with fewer stoppages, drastically reducing maintenance labour cost per tonne.

⚙️

Abrasion & Corrosion Ready

316 SS and PEEK withstand silica dust, corrosive mine water, acidic conditions, abrasive slurry, and constant washdowns, engineered for the conditions that destroy every other system.

Mine Performance

How Water Hydraulics Improve
Mine Efficiency

01 / Uptime

Reduced Downtime Across All Systems

No hazmat cleanup for leaks. Heat doesn't degrade fluid performance. Components last longer. Maintenance intervals extend drastically, fewer stoppages means more tonnes moved per shift.

02 / Safety

Safer Underground Working Conditions

No flammable fluid where heat accumulates, oxygen levels vary, ventilation is critical, and ignition sources must be minimized. Zero petroleum equals fewer hazards for every worker underground.

03 / Performance

Better Long-Stroke & High-Load Performance

Stable force without compressibility or viscosity sensitivity, improves gate accuracy, flow control, high-torque actuation, and lift-and-clamp reliability under the most demanding continuous loads.

04 / Economics

Simplified Equipment & Lower Total Cost

No large reservoirs, filtration units, cooling systems, or hazardous fluid storage. Over the lifecycle of a mine, savings in maintenance, fluid management, spill cleanup, and environmental compliance are substantial.
