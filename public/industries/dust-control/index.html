System Performance

How Water Hydraulics Improve
Dust Control Performance

1

Cleaner Systems and Improved Air Quality

No oil-dust sludge to clog ducts, filters, conveyors, nozzles, or housings. Keeps dust suppression systems performing at full efficiency, protecting both air quality and dust-reduction compliance targets.

2

Reduced Wear and Longer Component Life

Dust is inherently abrasive. Water hydraulics eliminate oil-dust bonding and reduce internal wear across all motion points, significantly extending the life of actuators, gates, and spray systems.

3

Lower Operating Costs

Fewer shutdowns, no spill cleanup, no oil changes, reduced filtration demand, and simplified maintenance routines. The savings compound quickly across facilities with high maintenance burdens.

4

Higher Reliability in the Harshest Environments

Conveyors, crushers, screens, and transfer points demand equipment that runs without fail. Water hydraulics provide strong, consistent performance under constant dust exposure, meeting the demands of 24/7 operations.

5

Better Alignment With Environmental and Safety Regulations

Dust control systems are installed to meet regulatory requirements. Removing oil strengthens compliance across air quality standards, fire safety codes, emission permits, and facility certification programs including CEPA and OSHA.
