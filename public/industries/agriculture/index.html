Why Water Hydraulics Strengthen Agriculture Compliance

  • Food Safety: Water hydraulics eliminate petroleum contamination of crops, irrigation water, and food processing equipment, supporting CFIA compliance and product quality standards.
  • Organic Certification: Non-petroleum hydraulic medium supports organic production requirements, helping operators meet certification standards for prohibited substances and inputs.
  • Soil Health Protection: Zero petroleum leakage protects long-term soil fertility, microbial activity, and farmland value, critical for generational farming operations.
  • Worker Safety: Elimination of oil mist and spray injuries improves safety in greenhouses, dairy parlours, and processing facilities, aligning with WorkSafeBC requirements.
  • Water Quality: Water hydraulics protect irrigation wells, drainage systems, and downstream water quality from petroleum contamination in agricultural watersheds.
