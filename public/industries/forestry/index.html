Why Water Hydraulics Strengthen Forestry Compliance

  • Fire Safety: Non-flammable hydraulic medium eliminates ignition risk during wildfire season, critical for operations in dry forest conditions and areas under fire bans.
  • Soil & Water Protection: Water hydraulics prevent petroleum contamination of forest soils, streams, and riparian zones, supporting fisheries habitat protection and watershed health.
  • Worker Safety: Elimination of oil mist, spray injuries, and slippery surfaces improves crew safety and aligns with WorkSafeBC injury prevention requirements.
  • Product Quality: Zero oil contamination protects finished lumber from staining, meeting export grade standards and customer specifications for appearance and cleanliness.
  • Sustainable Certification: Supports FSC, SFI, and CSA certification requirements for environmental stewardship, helping operators meet customer sustainability expectations.

Four Reasons Forestry Operations Are Making the Switch

Water hydraulics deliver measurable operational, safety, and financial benefits in logging, sawmill, and timber processing environments.

01 / Safety

Elimination of Fire Risk in High-Temperature, Debris-Rich Environments

Water hydraulics remove ignition sources near sawdust, wood chips, and hot cutting surfaces. Non-flammable actuation protects crews, equipment, and surrounding forest during summer operations and wildfire season.

02 / Environment

Protection of Forest Soils, Streams, and Salmon Habitat

Hydraulic leaks are eliminated completely. No petroleum contamination of riparian zones, spawning streams, or forest regeneration sites. Cleaner operations, stronger environmental stewardship, and simplified compliance.

03 / Reliability

Better Uptime in Remote Sites with Limited Resupply

Water hydraulic systems require only tap water to operate. No oil purchasing, trucking, or storage in remote locations. Fewer seal failures, less downtime, and simpler logistics for backcountry operations.

04 / Economics

Lower Lifecycle Cost, Rapid Payback for Operators

No hydraulic oil purchasing, filtration, fluid analysis, or hazardous disposal. Reduced insurance premiums from fire risk elimination. Cleaner product with no oil staining. System pays for itself quickly.
