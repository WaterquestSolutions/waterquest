Six Reasons

Why Water Hydraulics Fit
Every Hydro Power Facility

🌊

Zero Oil Leakage Into Rivers & Reservoirs

Any oil leak at a hydro facility is an environmental event. Water hydraulics remove this entirely, a leak introduces only water, with no containment, cleanup, reporting, or penalty required.

⚡

Safe in Turbine Halls & Underground Stations

Deep galleries carry high electrical loads. No oil means no fire hazard, no slippery surfaces, and safer confined-space entry for all maintenance crews in powerhouse basements.

💧

Corrosion-Resistant in High-Humidity Galleries

Constant moisture, mist, condensation, and temperature swings destroy oil-driven components. 316 SS and PEEK provide long-term durability in spillway chambers and draft tube zones.

🎯

Precise, Strong, Smooth Gate & Turbine Actuation

Gates and turbine controls require large forces, accurate throttling, and stable operation under high load. Water hydraulics deliver consistent pressure and reliable long-stroke performance.

🔧

Lower Maintenance for Remote & Lean Operations

No fluid changes, no filtration management, no contamination checks, no spill cleanup. Frees lean crews to focus on generating station priorities and planned maintenance windows.

🏗️

Clean Retrofit for Aging Hydro Infrastructure

Many stations are decades old. Water hydraulics provide a clean upgrade path for gate modernizations, turbine governor replacements, dam safety programs, and reliability improvements.
