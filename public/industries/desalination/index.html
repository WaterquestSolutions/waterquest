Why Water Hydraulics Strengthen Desalination Compliance

  • Brine Discharge Protection: Water hydraulics eliminate petroleum contamination of concentrate discharge, protecting coastal water quality and meeting Environment Canada discharge standards.
  • Membrane Integrity: Non-contaminating hydraulic medium eliminates oil fouling risk on RO membranes, protecting permeate quality and extending membrane service life.
  • Marine Ecosystem Protection: Zero oil leakage protects sensitive coastal ecosystems, fisheries habitat, and marine species from petroleum contamination at intake and discharge points.
  • Drinking Water Safety: Water hydraulics support potable water production standards, ensuring no petroleum introduction to treated water supply systems.
  • Corrosion Resistance: 316 stainless steel and PEEK construction withstands seawater exposure without material degradation, ensuring long-term reliability in coastal desalination plants.

Four Reasons Desalination Plants Are Making the Switch

Water hydraulics deliver measurable operational, environmental, and financial benefits in SWRO, BWRO, and membrane treatment facilities.

01 / Environmental

Elimination of Brine Discharge Contamination Risk

Water hydraulics remove petroleum from contact with seawater intake, brine concentrate, and coastal discharge. Zero-contamination actuation protects marine ecosystems and meets discharge regulations.

02 / Reliability

Better Uptime in High-Pressure, Corrosive Environments

316 SS and PEEK construction resists seawater corrosion and chloride attack. Extended seal life, fewer failures, and better uptime in demanding desalination service conditions.

03 / Water Quality

Protection of Membrane Performance and Permeate Quality

Water hydraulics eliminate oil fouling risk on RO membranes, protecting permeate quality, membrane service life, and product water specifications for potable water supply.

04 / Economics

Lower Lifecycle Cost, Faster Return on Investment

No hydraulic oil purchasing, filtration, or disposal. Reduced membrane replacement frequency. Simpler environmental compliance. System pays for itself quickly in desalination operations.
