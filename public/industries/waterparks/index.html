Performance Gains

How Water Hydraulics Improve
Aquatic Facility Performance

1

Cleaner Systems with No Chemical Interference

Chlorine, bromine, and pH-control agents damage oil systems over time. Water hydraulics eliminate fluid breakdown and chemical interaction entirely the hydraulic medium is fully compatible with the aquatic environment.

2

Higher Reliability in Pump Rooms, Tunnels & Buried Vaults

These spaces stay wet year-round from humidity, condensation, spray, and temperature variation. Water hydraulics perform reliably in all of these conditions without fluid degradation or seal failure from chemical attack.

3

Better Safety for Guests and Staff

No oil means no slip hazards in guest-accessible areas, no hydrocarbon residue in pool basins, no fire risk in enclosed pump rooms, and safer maintenance environments for the service teams who access these systems daily.

4

Lower Operating Costs Across the Facility

Savings accumulate from no oil filtration, no fluid disposal, fewer seal replacements, reduced downtime, and longer component life. Predictable maintenance schedules let operators plan better across seasons.

5

Fits Modern Environmental & Safety Standards

Municipalities and private operators need safer attractions, lower chemical load, reduced risk, sustainable operation, and fewer unplanned shutdowns. Water hydraulics support all of these goals without any performance trade-off.
