Reference data & calculation basis

Equations, assumptions and limits

A_e = πB²/4; A_r = π(B²−R²)/4; F_e = η(pA_e−p_back A_r); F_r = η(pA_r−p_back A_e)

1 bar acting on 1 cm² produces 10 N ideally. A margin of 1 means calculated extension force equals the entered factored load; it does not establish structural or holding safety.

Equations without conversion factors use coherent SI units: metres, square metres, cubic metres per second, pascals, newtons and watts. For hydraulic power pQ/600, use bar and L/min to obtain kW. Displacement formulas explicitly use cm³/rev and rpm. Percentages are divided by 100 before use as factors. The calculation keeps full numerical precision; results display up to five significant digits. Displayed digits do not establish measurement or model accuracy.
