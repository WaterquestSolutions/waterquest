Water Hydraulics Offshore: Start With One Auxiliary Circuit

Non-flammable fluid has obvious appeal on a platform. Prove one bounded function, its failure response and its spares, then scale.

Industry Applications6 min read

Download the worksheet for this guide (editable text) · Open the marine requirement register (CSV)

Written by WaterQuest Solutions · Updated 2 November 2026 · About the distributor

Design for the sea outside and the water inside

Hazardous-area and offshore approvals

Identify the classified zone, gas or dust group, temperature class and equipment protection requirements. IECEx certification and ATEX marking are distinct conformity frameworks; neither follows automatically from using water. Start with a bounded auxiliary function, such as an isolation-valve actuator, and define its complete approval route.

Water hydraulics deserve consideration where a defined function benefits from removing hydraulic oil and where the complete equipment can meet the offshore duty.

Choose a candidate function with a clear boundary

A platform may contain lifting, handling, valve actuation and other pressure-powered duties. Each has different requirements for motion, holding, readiness and emergency response.

The candidate should have a measurable duty and an identifiable reason to change. Examples of questions include whether a hydraulic line is difficult to contain, whether maintenance repeatedly exposes a sensitive location, or whether a renewal already requires major equipment replacement.

Turn load descriptions into operating states

A nameplate force or torque does not fully describe an offshore mechanism. Establish what happens during starting, normal movement, holding, reversing, stopping and recovery.

This state-based description gives the hydraulic designer something concrete to size and control. It also gives operators a way to review the proposal against real practice.

Do not assume seawater is the obvious working medium

Abundant seawater is not the same as a free, suitable hydraulic supply. A seawater circuit also creates intake, treatment and discharge interfaces.

The Water Hydraulics Company distinguishes purpose-designed water equipment and seawater pumping configurations in its published information. The practical lesson is to identify the fluid precisely on the enquiry and obtain confirmation for the offered assembly.

Corrosion control extends beyond a stainless label

Marine exposure can create local conditions that differ from a simple immersion test. Outokumpu's corrosion guidance explains why environment and mechanism must be considered alongside alloy selection.

For an offshore package, review external fasteners, brackets, tubing supports and inaccessible interfaces as well as pressure-containing parts.

Hydraulic power still depends on the complete path

The pump supplies pressure and flow, while valves, lines and actuators determine how that power reaches the task.

The Department of Energy's pumping-system guidance supports evaluating the complete system rather than a pump in isolation. Offshore, this approach has an additional practical benefit: reducing unnecessary energy losses can also reduce heat-management burdens. However, changing the working medium alone does not guarantee that result.

Define the response to loss of a shared service

Electrical power, control signals, inlet supply and cooling may be shared with other equipment. Losing one service can affect several functions at once.

For each important function, specify the intended response to loss of power or pressure. Holding position, stopping motion and moving to a designated position are different behaviours. A water-based medium does not automatically provide any of them. The chosen valves, mechanical arrangements and controls must support the required response, with evidence appropriate to the consequence. Any manual recovery provision also needs a clear authorized procedure and safe access.

Stored energy must remain visible during maintenance

A depressurized supply gauge does not necessarily prove that every isolated section is safe. CCOHS hazardous-energy guidance is a useful reminder that energy control includes stored and residual sources, not just switching off a motor.

The package should make the relevant isolation and verification points understandable to competent maintainers. Offshore access constraints can magnify the difficulty of a poorly located valve or test point.

Service logistics can dominate the ownership experience

A component with an attractive purchase price can become expensive if replacement requires specialist travel, unavailable tools or a long wait for a configuration-specific part.

Spares should be chosen through a criticality assessment rather than by purchasing one of everything.

Test representative conditions before relying on the package

Factory testing can verify important aspects of motion, leakage, controls and protective responses before the package reaches a difficult installation site. The test plan should state the loads, temperatures, fluid condition and operating sequences represented. A factory pass is valuable but does not erase site-specific integration work.

Where an uncertainty could affect acceptance, agree the method before purchase. This might involve a representative duty cycle or a defined test of a control transition, conducted under a safe authorized plan.

Keep environmental and hazardous-area claims precise

Removing hydraulic oil from a particular circuit changes one potential release pathway. It does not establish that the entire platform is oil-free, that discharged working water is harmless or that the equipment has a particular hazardous-area approval.

Any required approval must be checked against current documentation for the exact equipment and application.

A practical offshore decision connects benefit and readiness

The most useful proposal brings together five things: a defined duty, an identified oil-exposure benefit, compatible equipment, a verified response to important operating states and a workable service arrangement. A technically capable actuator can still be a poor project choice if it depends on a supply that cannot be maintained, just as an appealing environmental statement cannot compensate for unproven motion performance.
