Engineering Design

Built From First Principles. Not Adapted From Oil.

The P1 uses purpose-designed internal components for water service. Carbon-fibre-reinforced PEEK interfaces help the moving parts work with water as the medium. Match the selected construction and operating conditions to your supply so the pump supports the required duty without oil in its working circuit.

316L Stainless Steel Housing

316L stainless construction provides a starting point for water-service selection. Review the actual water chemistry, temperature and seal package, with application-specific documentation for marine or food-related installations.

CF30-PEEK Internal Components

Use the performance data and service instructions for the exact model at the intended duty. Efficiency, wear and replacement intervals depend on operating conditions and the selected component construction.

Reduced hydraulic-oil exposure

Remove hydraulic oil from this working circuit and the oil-handling tasks that go with it. Specify the working water and service routine so the installation remains practical to operate.

Compact Footprint

Miniature gear pump geometry in a package small enough for embedded HPU installations and tight enclosures. The P1 fits where larger pump lines simply cannot, making it ideal for mobile, subsea, and lab-scale systems.

Two Displacement Options

Choose 0.8 cc/rev (0.0488 in³/rev) (0.049 in³/rev) for precision low-flow work or 1.2 cc/rev (0.0732 in³/rev) (0.073 in³/rev) where higher throughput is required. Both share identical mounting geometry, allowing in-field displacement changes without repiping. Speed ranges from 500 to 1750 rpm.

Low Maintenance Lifecycle

Confirm inlet, case-drain and service requirements for the selected configuration. Cavitation, seal life and filtration remain design and maintenance considerations; review them against the actual operating conditions.

HPU Integration

The P1 Inside a WaterQuest HPU

The P1 is the heart of WaterQuest's compact HPU offerings. Paired with an electric motor, reservoir, filtration block, and manifold, it ships as a complete hydraulic power unit ready for connection. This is the starting point for customers who want a full turnkey water hydraulic system without engineering a custom circuit from scratch.

For larger applications requiring more flow or redundancy, multiple P1 units can be run in parallel within a single HPU enclosure. This modularity also provides graceful degradation; if one pump requires service, the circuit stays operational.

Explore WaterQuest HPU Packages

P1 HPU Configuration Options

The following configurations are available when ordering the P1 as part of a WaterQuest HPU package. All units use tap water or softened seawater as the working fluid with no additives required.

  • Single P1 with TEFC motor, 1 litre (0.26 US gal) reservoir and bypass relief valve
  • Dual P1 parallel configuration for 4.2 L/min (1.11 US gpm) maximum throughput
  • Integrated pressure transducer and 4-20mA output for SCADA connection
  • Variable frequency drive (VFD) coupling for proportional flow control
  • 316L stainless manifold block with up to 4 work ports
  • Optional inline filtration to 5 micron (197 µin) (0.197 mil) for high-pressure circuits
  • NEMA 4X or IP67 enclosure for outdoor and marine environments
