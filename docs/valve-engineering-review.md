# Engineering review items

These items are retained as concise configuration checks in the public pages. No unverified certification or availability claims were added.

- DN16 directional valves: the operating range is 0–100 bar although the maximum inlet entry is 160 bar. Match the pilot arrangement and pilot pressure separately.
- Electronic flow control: the DN3 and DN6 metering ranges differ between the descriptive text and ordering table. Confirm the required range at the intended pressure differential.
- Electronic pressure control: DN6 flow entries differ. Confirm flow range, feedback signal and control tuning; DN12 manifold ordering details require confirmation.
- DN12 pilot-operated check: verify the flow coefficient pair and pressure loss at the intended duty. The site does not repeat the inconsistent coefficient pair.
- Relief valves: the DN12 capacity differs between entries. Confirm the required duty and final configuration. Resolve any required safety or pressure-equipment approvals separately. No certification claim is made on the page.
- Hose burst valves: check compatibility between the listed maximum flow, adjustment range and the stated 1.5-times-system-flow trip setting.
- Solenoid actuation: match each card programme, coil and power supply as an assembly. The standard control system voltage is distinct from the coil energisation sequence.
- ST5-Q-NN: verify software access, programme package, drive availability, motor compatibility and communication requirements before supply.
- Ordering guide: special relief pressure classes require confirmation. Nominal code size and pressure class do not replace the operating limits of the selected product. Only listed examples are decoded; arbitrary combinations are not offered as validated products.
- Zone valves: confirm porting, complete actuation configuration and any required water-mist system approvals.

Illustrations are for product presentation and planning. Confirm final installation dimensions and settings for the ordered configuration.
